Haelka Breen is the quartermaster who controls the rationing of food and supplies in [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]] on behalf of the [[Guardians of the Great Work]], operating out of the [[The Guardian Compound|Guardian Compound]]. Bribes and favoritism reportedly run rampant under her watch.
