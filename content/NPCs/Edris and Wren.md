Edris and Wren are twin siblings who run a black-market supply network in [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]], smuggling food and technology past patrols run by the [[Guardians of the Great Work]] to reach starving families in the district. They operate as part of [[The Saltborn]] resistance.
