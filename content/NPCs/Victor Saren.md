Victor Saren is the digital CEO of [[Omnitek Holdings]], encountered by [[Felix Champlin]] in the flashback recounting his origin in Episode 9, "Brother's Keeper: Felix's Story." Saren appeared as a digital presence rather than a man in the room, manifesting to defend Omnitek's orbital data vault above [[_Polyphn|Polyphn]] with a set of telescopic restraint arms when Felix, his brother [[Petey Champlin]], and the rest of their crew — [[Nyx Verhoeven]] and [[Jett Harlow]] — broke in.

The vault held evidence of [[Project Red Wing|Operation Red Wing]], an Omnitek program that purchased prisoners and used them as test subjects for digitizing human consciousness. During the heist, Petey's own consciousness was captured and trapped within Omnitek's servers. Felix escaped, but was forced to burn his old identity, change his name, and later infiltrate Omnitek itself hoping to find a way to rescue his brother. Killing Victor Saren has been one of Felix's core, driving motivations ever since.
