Victor Saren is the CEO of [[Omnitek Holdings]]. [[Felix Champlin]] encountered him in the flashback recounting his origin in Episode 9, "Brother's Keeper: Felix's Story," when Felix, his brother [[Petey Champlin]], and the rest of their crew ([[Nyx Verhoeven]] and [[Jett Harlow]]) broke into Omnitek's orbital data vault above [[_Polyphn|Polyphn]]. Saren confronted them there, deploying a set of telescopic restraint arms to defend the vault.

The vault held evidence of [[Project Red Wing|Operation Red Wing]], an Omnitek program that purchased prisoners and used them as test subjects for digitizing human consciousness. During the heist, Petey's own consciousness was captured and trapped within Omnitek's servers. Felix escaped, but was forced to burn his old identity, change his name, and later infiltrate Omnitek itself hoping to find a way to rescue his brother. Killing Victor Saren has been one of Felix's core, driving motivations ever since.

Saren's own office turned up aboard [[Prometheon Technologies|Prometheon Station]] in Episode 32, immaculate and unused, worked by [[Felix Champlin|Felix]] and the crew while trapped in a time loop there. A framed photograph on the desk showed Saren shaking hands with a diplomat, an older man clearly recognizable as the same face the crew knows as [[Viktor Strake]], just about forty years younger. Chat logs recovered from the terminal showed Saren communicating remotely with a virtual intelligence designated V.Mistretta-4 over several weeks, authorizing a station activation against her explicit warning that a single "ear" wasn't strong enough to safely answer whatever the station's gyroscope was reaching toward. The final logged message, timestamped "day 23, 0347," reads only "begin," with no entries after it.
