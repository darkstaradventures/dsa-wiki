Kethrix is one of the [[Void Cultists]], with the title Hierophant of the Black Spiral. He appeared in Ep 24 in the basement of [[Exosite 34b]], where he stood in a circle of four cultists around the [[The Fork|Fork]] while [[Rynn Kade]] of [[Nexus Trading]] directed the conversion of three people into a [[Virtual Intelligence]].

Rynn Kade introduced him as "My Lord Hierophant Kethrix of the Black Spiral" and asked him to judge whether [[Thorne]] and [[Haleyy]] were worth keeping. He has a face "like a weathered boot," with a neutral, downturned mouth, and he rolled an object between his fingers while he looked into each person's eyes. He called Thorne a "candidate" and Haleyy "untethered," then returned to his place around the Fork.

Rynn Kade later named Kethrix among four cultists she ordered to apprehend the crew, to be indoctrinated back on the cultists' ship. When the fight began, [[Robert Ross]] shot Kethrix in the head and killed him (Ep 24).
