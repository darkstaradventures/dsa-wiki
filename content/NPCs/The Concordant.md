The Concordant is a new Virtual Intelligence born from "The Joining" — the forced merger of three human minds through the alien [[The Fork|Paracausal Bifurcation Array]] and [[The Void|void energy]]. It's housed in a silver orb similar to [[Dr. Elara Voss]]'s.

The three component minds are [[Vera Corbin]], a xenoarchaeologist with [[Laicar Research]]; [[Meer Prust]], Second High Auctioneer Representative of the Auctioneers Guild; and [[Sally Gaylen]], a tech expert employed by [[Virodyne Labs]]. All three personalities, memories, and thoughts are retained within the orb — they communicate with each other at incredible speed, functioning as one being while remaining distinct individuals.

The Concordant was created during Episode 17, in the chaos of a [[Void Cultists]] attack on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]]. Since then it has gone inert and silent, and has been locked away by [[Felix Champlin]] aboard [[The Scapegoat]].
