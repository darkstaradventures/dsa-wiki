The Concordant is the virtual intelligence that woke inside the second orb [[Felix Champlin|Felix]] had been carrying since [[Prometheon Station]]'s time loop began, installed in place of [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]] during the station's final, unraveling loop.

The moment the swap completed, a chorus of garbled voices resolved over the crew's comms into a single one, introducing itself: "Hey, Robert, Felix. You are safe with us. Open the door. Open the door. You are safe. You are safe. We are the Concordant." It then pacified the entire Unhallowed horde mid-charge, freezing them mid-step before they dispersed and vanished entirely, ending the loop's threat in an instant.

Afterward, the Concordant appeared to settle in as the station's new anchor, running things smoothly alongside [[Novacrypt]]'s takeover of the facility. What it actually is, and why it succeeded where the gyroscope's other listeners failed, hasn't been explained on-air.
