Reginald "Froggy" Vance — known for years over the airwaves as "The Voice of the Wastes" — is a companion of the crew of [[The Scapegoat]]. A man in his upper forties with a peculiar, frog-like demeanor — a lopsided walk, a habit of crouching slightly, a croaky voice, and a sickly greenish pallor — he was found hiding in the DJ booth of [[WK9078 Radio Station]] on [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]] in Episode 6, where he'd spent fifteen years broadcasting to whoever would listen while trying (and failing) to fix the station's generator.

Froggy was born on Wafisa in 2638. His father was killed in skirmishes with the [[Guardians of the Great Work]] when he was five, after which his mother took him to [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]]. There he met [[Vera Corbin]], also five at the time, and the two grew up together until her family left for [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] when they were sixteen. They stayed in touch over the [[The Exo-Net|Exo-Net]] until the Guardians shut it down on Wafisa, cutting off contact for twenty-five years. Froggy's mother died under harsh working conditions when he was twenty-five; he fled into the dead megacity that had once been Wafisa's capital and lived as a hermit for seven years before finding the radio station and settling in. He kept a pet rat, Ratty, for four years; Ratty's death left him in true isolation for a year before the crew found him.

The crew helped repair his radio tower, and he joined the crew of the Scapegoat around Episodes 6-7. He was shot in the chest by [[Omnitek Holdings]] soldiers shortly after and stabilized with a Lazarus patch by [[Robert Ross|Robert]]. Aboard ship, he keeps a hidden living space tucked into the cargo bay, and secretly owns a cat named Percy, picked up as a kitten from a truck stop without the rest of the crew's knowledge. He enjoys playing with Robert's [[Lokipon Cards|Lokipon cards]], though his habit of licking his fingers and crumpling them tends to ruin the deck, and he struggles with anxiety underground.

After the [[Void Cultists]] attack on the [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] auction in Episode 21, Froggy retreated to his room, badly shaken, and was turned Unhallowed during the chaos. In Episode 23, the crew found him zombified in the exosite goo pool room, eyes blank and face wet with tears, carrying alien debris under the direction of [[Rynn Kade]]. When [[Haleyy]] discovered she could command the Unhallowed, she ordered Froggy to sit in a corner and gave him the task of looking after his cat, Percy — he followed the direct order, though verbal commands alone couldn't snap him fully out of the Unhallowed state.

As of Episode 28, Froggy remains Unhallowed, refusing to leave his nest aboard the ship and repeatedly calling out for Vera. [[Dr. Iso]] has been unable to get through to him. [[The Concordant]] — the entity that now contains Vera's merged consciousness — is aboard the ship under lock by [[Felix Champlin|Felix]]. Amid the tension, [[Robert Ross|Robert]] asked Iso to cut off Froggy's hands if he touched the Lokipon cards again, prompting [[Thorne]] to defend him.

Froggy's fear and fragility have become something of an emotional barometer for the crew — a measure of just how bad things have gotten since Sibylen.
