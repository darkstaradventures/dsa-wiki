Brick was hired muscle working for [[Mara Vex]] on [[_Polyphn|Polyphn]], and grew up in the same Block Six district of Hock Bay Prefecture that Mara did. He had "BRIK" tattooed across his knuckles and warned Thorn Drax that he'd use them if Thorn brought Mara to harm.

Brick drove the getaway gravcar during the alley ambush with two Nexus Trading enforcers, then lost their pursuers with a jump off a construction ramp into a parking garage. He escorted the group to Dr. Sial's clinic and later stood watch at the relay tower outside Hock Bay Prefecture. When Nexus Trading forces surrounded the tower, he fought alongside Mara, Thorn, and Kano Rix at ground level while Thorn climbed to activate the relay. He was killed in that firefight, his body found alongside Kano Rix's after Nexus forces overwhelmed them.
