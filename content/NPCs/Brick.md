Brick was a former enforcer for a [[Bloc 6]] gang on [[_Polyphn|Polyphn]] — a branch of the [[Drift Rats]] — before becoming [[Mara Vex]]'s bodyguard. Orphaned young, he was often fed and clothed by Mara's family; when a maglev crash killed them, Mara turned to him for help, and he left his gang behind to become her protector.

Broad-shouldered and well over six feet tall, with a shaved head, thick beard, and arms covered in scars and gang tattoos, Brick wore a reinforced tactical vest and carried a battered dog tag around his neck — his grandfather's, from an old war on Polyphn. He was fiercely loyal, blunt and plainspoken, pragmatic to a fault, and visibly haunted by guilt over his gang days; he rarely let Mara out of his sight, and had "BRIK" tattooed across his knuckles.

During the alley ambush and the chase that followed, Brick drove the getaway gravcar. He fought [[Nexus Trading]] forces at the base of [[The Relay Tower]] and was killed in the Nexus assault on the tower.
