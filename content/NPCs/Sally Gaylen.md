Sally Gaylen is a tech expert working for [[Virodyne Labs]], present on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] for the auction of [[Exosite 34b]]. She carried insider knowledge of Virodyne's operations.

During the [[Void Cultists]] attack on the auction in Episode 17, Gaylen was captured along with [[Vera Corbin]] and [[Meer Prust]]. All three were wired to [[The Fork|Paracausal Bifurcation Array]], the alien device at the heart of the exosite, and their minds were merged by void energy into a single new intelligence: [[The Concordant]].
