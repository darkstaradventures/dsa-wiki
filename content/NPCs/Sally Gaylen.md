Sally Gaylen is named among the captives taken when [[Void Cultists]] attacked and overran the [[Exosite 34b]] dig site on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] following the auction.

<!-- REVIEW: her employer (Virodyne Labs) and role as a tech expert are not directly confirmed in the transcript passages checked; Virodyne was one of the corporations attending the auction, but Gaylen's own affiliation was not stated on-air in what was reviewed. -->

She was taken to the alien device known as the Fork (the Paracausal Bifurcation Array), along with [[Vera Corbin]] and [[Meer Prust]], and the three were merged into a single virtual intelligence housed in a silver orb, which the crew has since referred to informally as "Vera."
