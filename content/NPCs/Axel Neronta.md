Axel Neronta is the eldest son of [[Roger Neronta]]. He works at [[Roger's Billiards]] on [[_Camiri 4|Camiri 4]], serving drinks and helping run the establishment. Like his father, he's friendly and generous, and he's something of a local expert on the station's music history, trading opinions on old bands with anyone who'll listen — including the memorable lines "Gravity Well was my grandfather's time" and "Where Gravity Well walked, Nebula Cult rocked."
