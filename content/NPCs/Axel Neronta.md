Axel Neronta is the son of [[Roger Neronta]]. He works at [[Roger's Billiards]] on [[_Camiri 4|Camiri 4]], serving drinks and helping run the establishment. He's friendly and generous, pouring the crew free rounds and unknowingly showing them how the bar's hand-sensor storage door worked when he walked through it to fetch supplies. He's also a fan of the station's music history, calling out old bands like Gravity Well and Nebula Cult, and telling the crew, "Gravity Well was my grandfather's time," and "Where Gravity Well walked, Nebula Cult rocked."
