William Sallerist is the man [[Thorne]] spent years believing had destroyed his life. He was formerly the managing director of [[Draxus Goods]], Thorne's family company, after [[Nexus Trading]] absorbed it.

Sallerist had been running a smuggling ring within Nexus Trading's operations. When [[Thalia Draxus]] planted evidence implicating both Thorne and Sallerist — hoping to remove them both from the company — Sallerist managed to shift the blame entirely onto Thorne, who was sentenced to ten years in prison. Sallerist then took over as managing director of Draxus Goods and pushed Thalia out entirely, running the company as a Nexus Trading subsidiary for roughly nine years.

About a year before Episode 13, Sallerist was declared dead in a maglev crash at [[Bloc 6]] that killed twelve residents and five other occupants — Nexus officers moved in afterward to clean up survivors and complete the cover-up. In truth, he had faked his death and disappeared into hiding.

In Episode 13, "Nexus of Betrayal: Thorne's Story," Thorne uncovered proof from a data cache at [[The Relay Tower]] that Sallerist was still alive — proof that also revealed the deeper truth that it was actually [[Thalia Draxus]], not Sallerist alone, who had framed him. As of that episode, Sallerist remains missing, his current whereabouts unknown to the crew.
