Rynn Kade was an agent of [[Nexus Trading]] who first appeared in Episode 21 on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]]. Already "untethered" — aligned with the [[Void Cultists]] but not yet turned Unhallowed — she attempted to secure [[Exosite 34b]] through the auction. When she failed to win it, her cultist allies attacked.

The crew later found her within the exosite complex, directing Unhallowed laborers. She was killed in Episode 24 at Exosite 34b, shot by [[Robert Ross]] after she put a bullet through [[Thorne]]'s chest, leaving him at the brink of death.
