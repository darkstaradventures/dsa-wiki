Brother Calyx was a void cultist stationed at [[Exosite 34b]] on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]], part of the [[Void Cultists]]. He referred to himself in the third person by his cult title — "Old Brother Calyx has got himself a cat. This is the best day ever." — and carried himself with a soldier's bearing, dressed in heavy cult armor.

When the crew arrived at Exosite 34b, Brother Calyx was crouched petting [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Percy]], the crew's cat. Combat broke out, and after his partner was killed he tried to surrender, offering to explain himself and inviting the crew to join the Void Cult, calling it a source of purpose. When he refused to reveal why [[Rynn Kade]] was present at the site — declaring he'd rather die than betray the cult — [[Haleyy]] killed him.
