Nyx Verhoeven, known in the underworld as Silentpaw, is a cat burglar, a perfectionist who dislikes violence. She appeared in a flashback in Episode 9, played by Steph, repaying a debt to [[Felix Champlin]], who had once saved her life by rerouting medical drones to her.

Nyx has a signature calling card: she leaves small mouse figurines, nicknamed "Little Mice," at the scenes of her heists. During the vault job in Episode 9, [[Jett Harlow]] admitted to her that he'd been secretly stealing the mice back after jobs and taking credit for her work instead of crediting Nyx, which was why someone else got credit for an earlier job on Wafisa.

She was last seen during the escape from a data vault heist.
