Vera Corbin is a xenoarchaeologist, the last remaining employee of [[Laicar Research]] on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]], and an old friend of [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Froggy]] from childhood. Excitable and driven, she was in her upper forties when the crew met her.

She was born on [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]] and moved to Sibylen with her family as a teenager. She began working for Laicar Research young, which gave her access to [[The Exo-Net|the Exo-Net]] and let her reconnect with Froggy, contact that was later severed for twenty-five years after the [[Guardians of the Great Work|Guardians]] shut the network down on Wafisa. Over the following decades she helped build Laicar Research and became a part owner, but by the time the crew arrived the company was down to about five employees and going bankrupt.

The crew met Vera in Episode 14 at Laicar's research station on Sibylen, after Froggy (whom they had kept alive since he was shot in Episode 7) asked them to deliver her a message. It was an emotional reunion; she still calls him "Reginald." Vera had discovered [[Exosite 34b]], a buried alien quarantine facility, and had been excavating it for about eighteen months. She identified alien inscriptions in the facility as patterns associated with medical or healing structures, and saw the site's upcoming auction as Laicar Research's last chance at survival. She struck a contract with [[Thorne]], giving [[Scapegoat Solutions LLC]] a cut of any auction discoveries in exchange for the crew's help. When the exosite's door activated for the first time, she marveled: *"This might be the first example of alien technology actually activating. You have no idea what this means."*

In Episode 15, she helped the crew explore the deeper levels of the quarantine facility. But in Episode 17, during the auction, [[Void Cultists]] attacked and captured her along with [[Meer Prust]] and [[Sally Gaylen]]. All three were wired to [[The Fork|Paracausal Bifurcation Array]] and merged by void energy into a single new intelligence, [[The Concordant]].
