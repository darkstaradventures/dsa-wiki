Thalia Draxus is [[Thorne]]'s mother and the former CEO of [[Draxus Goods]], the family company he grew up expecting to inherit. Sharp, calculating, and driven above all by protecting the company she built, she has proven willing to make devastating unilateral choices in its name.

Years before the crew's journey began, [[William Sallerist]] was running a smuggling ring inside Draxus Goods' operations. Worried about her son's increasingly reckless involvement, Thalia planted evidence implicating both Thorne and Sallerist, intending for both to be removed from the picture. The plan backfired badly: Thorne took the full ten-year sentence while Sallerist escaped blame, then used the company's collapsed credibility to sell it off cheap to [[Nexus Trading]] once he took over as managing director. Thalia spent the following decade trying to reclaim what she'd lost.

Thalia resurfaced in Episode 13, "Nexus of Betrayal: Thorne's Story." She anonymously contacted Thorne with a cipher key and instructions to meet [[Mara Vex]], having also arranged Mara's side of the encounter. She then confronted Thorne atop [[The Relay Tower]], dressed head to toe in black, and revealed the truth he had never known: that it was her, not Sallerist alone, who had framed him. She claimed to have spent the ten years since trying to undo what she'd done, and offered Thorne her resources and information going forward. In the chaos that followed, she vanished up the tower's stairs before the fighting ended. Her means of escape remains a mystery to this day.
