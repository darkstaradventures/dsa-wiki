Dr. Ilon Speth is a retired xenobiologist who runs the med bay at [[_Scrapjacks|Scrap Jack's Refueling and Grub]]. Quirky and a little scattered when the crew first met her, she grew progressively more raspy, intoxicated, and unhinged over the course of their stay at the station — one of the more memorable characters to come out of the Scrapjack's arc.
