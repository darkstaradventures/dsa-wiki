Dr. Ilon Speth is a retired xenobiologist who runs the med bay at [[_Scrapjacks|Scrap Jack's Refueling and Grub]]. Quirky and a little scattered when the crew first met her, she grew progressively more raspy and unhinged, drinking more heavily over the course of their stay at the station. She became one of the more memorable characters of the Scrapjack's arc.
