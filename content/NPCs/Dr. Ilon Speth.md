Dr. Speth runs the med bay at [[_Scrapjacks|Scrap Jack's Refueling and Grub]]. Prickly and insistent on being addressed by her title, she clashed repeatedly with Felix over payment, propriety, and her qualifications, and grew more erratic and heavier-drinking over the course of the crew's stay at the station. During the void cult's takeover of the station, the crew tied and gagged her to keep her quiet while they hid out in the med bay.
