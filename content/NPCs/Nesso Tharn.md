Nesso Tharn was a former ship captain turned second-in-command of the [[Void Cultists]] cult operating out of [[_Scrapjacks|Scrapjacks]]. A bald, pale man with piercing blue eyes, abnormally tall and gangly, he was tactical and stern, testing the faith of those around him like a drill sergeant.

Tharn confronted the crew at Scrapjacks in Episodes 10 and 11. The encounter ended with his death — killed by [[Haleyy]], who punched him and drove his face into cement.
