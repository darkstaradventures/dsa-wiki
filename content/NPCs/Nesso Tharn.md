Nesso Tharn, addressed by fellow cultists as "Brother Neso," was a senior member of the [[Void Cultists]] cult operating out of [[_Scrapjacks|Scrapjacks]]. Tall, gangly, and pale, he spoke with a calm, indoctrinating manner toward captives and was treated with deference by other cultists.

Tharn confronted the crew at Scrapjacks in Episodes 10 and 11. The encounter ended with his death: Robert Ross pulled him off balance, exposing the back of his skull, and [[Haleyy]] drove her fist into it, cracking the cement floor as his face hit the ground.
