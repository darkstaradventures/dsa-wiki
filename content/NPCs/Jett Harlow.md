Jett Harlow is a gunslinger with a deadly calm and a darkly witty streak — reputation enough that he's known as "the Viper." He appeared in a flashback to a data vault heist, taking the job for the credits and because [[Nyx Verhoeven]] vouched for him. In the flashback, he was voiced by guest performer Doja. He was last seen alive during the crew's escape from the vault.
