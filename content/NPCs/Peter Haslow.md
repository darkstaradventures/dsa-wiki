Peter Haslow is a man in a silver suit who frequents [[The Vent]] bar in [[_The Spigot|The Spigot]]. Bushy-bearded and bald, with wiry pale arms, he's a competent mechanic who repairs bots in the dunes and scribes and installs ownership modules. Described by [[Dr. Iso]] as a self-described big-time snitch and "huge rat," he's nonetheless a nice guy at heart — and the two don't get along. He ended up stuck on the Spigot years ago when a temporary job turned into a stable one and he could no longer afford the fare home.

He first appeared in Episode 25, when [[Thorne]] approached him at the Vent using the cover identity Tyson Fildar of Goodbot Electronics. Haslow agreed to sell information for 500 credits: the next autonomous shipment of ownership modules was due to arrive at Helium Docks #7 at 3am, unloaded entirely by bots with no human attendants. He was caught in a grenade blast at the Vent but survived, and was being questioned by Regulators as Thorne left the scene.
