Percy is an orange cat that lives aboard [[The Scapegoat]]. The crew did not know about the cat until [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Froggy]] mentioned her in Ep 14.

Froggy said he got a cat because the ship had rats, and that he picked her up at a truck stop, where there was a litter of cats and he chose the cutest one. She was a kitten when he brought her aboard and had since grown into a cat. He called her a great mouser. Froggy keeps a sleeping space in the cargo bay, and he said he and Percy spend all their time there. When Thorne mentioned that Froggy had chewed through cords in the cargo bay, Froggy said Percy had chewed the cables while teething. [[Haleyy]] accused Percy of drinking the orange juice, which Froggy denied (Ep 14). Froggy refers to Percy as "she" (Ep 14), and the GM later refers to the cat as "he" (Ep 23). In Ep 17, [[Robert Ross|Robert]] threatened to throw Froggy and his cat down a hole, and Froggy asked if he was threatening his cat.

The crew treated the cat as imaginary until Ep 22, when Robert spotted an orange streak running off. Haleyy tracked the cat to Froggy's nest in the cargo bay, where it hissed at her. The nest also held Robert's knife, last seen when the crew left [[_Scrapjacks|Scrapjacks]], a set of torn-up [[Lokipon Cards|Lokipon]] cards, and an empty orange juice container. [[Thorne]] said the cat had been real all along (Ep 22). The cat was also near the hole cut in the ship's hull, and Robert blocked it from escaping through it (Ep 22). Thorne petted the cat at the start of Ep 23.

In Ep 23, Percy followed the crew into the Void Cultist exosite. Two cultists, one of whom later gave his name as [[Marun]], were crouched petting the cat. Haleyy said she wanted to punch the man holding Percy, and declared that the cat was no longer Froggy's but the Scapegoat's. During the fight the cat jumped from Froggy onto Haleyy's shoulders, and afterward went to Froggy and fell asleep with him. Haleyy told Froggy his mission was to take care of Percy (Ep 23). In Ep 27, Percy came out while the crew was in the mess hall, and [[Felix Champlin|Felix]] glared at the cat. In Ep 30, Froggy came out of his den holding Percy.
