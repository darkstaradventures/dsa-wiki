Joran Red Veyne is a former [[Guardians of the Great Work|Guardian]] soldier turned rebel leader, and the crew's main contact within [[The Saltborn]] resistance in [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]]. Charismatic and resourceful, he built an organization after defecting from the Guardians, drawing capable, skilled people to his cause. Under his leadership, hope has begun to take root in Blackharbor despite the Guardians' grip on the district.
