Meer Prust is the Second High Auctioneer Representative for [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]], a short, heavyset man with a white Hungarian mustache who wears the ceremonial robes of the [[Auctioneers Guild]]. An expert in bureaucracy and InterCorporate law, he holds offices in [[_The Spigot|The Spigot]].

He first appeared in Episode 21, arriving during [[Viktor Strake]]'s interrogation of the crew. Using his authority, he demanded Strake release [[Vera Corbin|Dr. Corbin]] as the auction representative for [[Laicar Research]], then backed [[Thorne]]'s legal argument to secure the release of the entire crew for 24 hours — quietly telling Strake afterward, "After the auction, they're all yours."

Prust went on to conduct the auction for [[Exosite 34b]]. When [[Void Cultists]] attacked during the auction, he was captured and wired into the alien Fork alongside [[Vera Corbin]] and [[Sally Gaylen]], his consciousness merging into the new entity known as [[The Concordant]].
