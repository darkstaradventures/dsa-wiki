Petey is [[Felix Champlin]]'s younger brother, a journalist and former field medic who served in the Doppler Skirmishes. He's referred to here by his brother's surname for convenience; "Champlin" is the identity Felix built for himself only after Petey's death, so Petey never actually went by that name, and the brothers' real birth surname has never been revealed. He worked for Blackout Press under the pen name "Dox Ryker," acting as a mole inside [[Omnitek Holdings]] to investigate their prisoner conversion program, and spent several months at Omnitek itself under a "research assistant" cover identity.

His story was revealed in a flashback in Episode 9. During a heist on an orbital data vault, Petey was killed by a device that copies a person's consciousness and destroys the body in the process. His body died, but an AI copy of his mind was left behind, trapped within Omnitek's servers and subjected to nightmare testing simulations as part of a covert Omnitek program. Notably, Petey's copy is an AI, a copied consciousness, rather than a true consciousness transfer like the one that created [[Dr. Elara Voss]], where the original person continues to exist.

Freeing Petey is a central driver of [[Felix Champlin|Felix]]'s motivations.
