Harlan Rigg grew up in the cargo trade, hardened by years running freight on the fringes of settled space. Reliable and hard to rattle, he pilots a ship for Draxus Goods out of the auxiliary lot in [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]], working alongside [[Callum Vex]].
