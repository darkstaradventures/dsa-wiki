Callum Vex is a no-nonsense hauler for Draxus Goods, flying routes between [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]], [[_Polyphn|Polyphn]], and Zephryss III alongside his partner, [[Harlan Rigg]]. He's seen it all, from deep-space breakdowns to shady cargo deals, and mostly just wants to get paid and stay in the air. He can typically be found around the auxiliary lot at [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]].
