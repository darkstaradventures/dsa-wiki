Torik Rustclaw Sarr, known simply as Rustclaw, was a hulking, heavily scarred figure who grew up in the industrial zones of [[_Camiri 4|Camiri 4]]. His nickname came from his brutal fighting style and a mechanical cybernetic claw. Known for his strength and commanding presence, he led the [[Drift Rats]] gang and fancied himself something of a poet ("Docking Bay 72, look at me, I'm a poet.")

The crew first encountered Rustclaw in Episode 1 and did him a favor. In Episode 2, once the job was done, he betrayed them, attempting to steal their ship as they prepared to leave. [[Robert Ross]] killed him with a critical headshot from a combat rifle during the ensuing space chase.
