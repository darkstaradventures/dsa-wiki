Roger Neronta is a guard with the [[Neon Fangs]] and owner of Roger's, a pool hall on [[_Camiri 4|Camiri 4]] in [[_Neon Fangs Territory|Neon Fangs Territory]].

His wife works the bar, and his eldest son [[Axel Neronta|Axel]] tends bar alongside her. Roger appeared in Episode 1, greeting the crew warmly and pointing them toward the pool hall, telling Haleyy as they parted: *"My friend, whatever's happening, it will get better. It will. Just stay with your friends. They're a good lot."* In Episode 2, at the pool hall, it was Axel, not Roger, who returned the crew's credits, since the first round was supposed to be on the house.
