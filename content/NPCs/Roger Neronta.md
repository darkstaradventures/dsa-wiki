Roger Neronta is a guard with the [[Neon Fangs]] and owner of Roger's Billiards, a five-table pool hall on [[_Camiri 4|Camiri 4]] in [[_Neon Fangs Territory|Neon Fangs Territory]]. Extremely generous — sometimes to a fault, according to his wife — he gives back to his community more than he probably should.

His wife Cecelia works the bar, and his eldest son [[Axel Neronta|Axel]] loves rock music. Roger appeared in Episode 1, where he returned credits to [[Haleyy]] out of pure kindness, telling her: *"My friend, whatever's happening, it will get better. It will. Just stay with your friends. They're a good lot."*
