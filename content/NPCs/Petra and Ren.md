Petra and Ren are two researchers sealed inside a lab deep in [[Prometheon Technologies|Prometheon Station]], trapped alongside [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]]'s orb behind a locked door that floods with a corrosive gas on a fixed schedule. Petra talks to the crew through the porthole using a notepad pressed to the glass, since neither side can hear the other over the intercom. By her own count, she's died to the gas twenty times, watching the same short window reset over and over with no memory of the crew having ever come before.

She explained that the station's gas is tied to a dead man's switch: the station's CEO, [[Victor Saren|Saren]], has to personally clear it from his office terminal every day, and nobody has been able to since he vanished when the loop began. She pointed the crew toward his office as the only way to stop it, and toward [[The Listeners]] who run the station as the ones who might know why any of this is happening. Ren, more frantic and single-minded than Petra, cared about only one thing: getting the gas turned off, telling the crew to forget the rest of what's happening in the station and focus there first.

On the loop where the crew finally reached Saren's terminal, [[Felix Champlin|Felix]] made the call to trigger the gas himself rather than wait, in order to force through the access he needed. Petra and Ren died in the resulting blast, for real this time, over [[Thorne]]'s objection.
