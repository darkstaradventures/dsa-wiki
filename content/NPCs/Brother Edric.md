Brother Edric is a priest who runs [[The Iron Chapel]] in [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]], sheltering rebels within its walls. He is affiliated with [[The Saltborn]], and while the [[Guardians of the Great Work]] tolerate the chapel's existence, persistent rumors hold that he aids the resistance.
