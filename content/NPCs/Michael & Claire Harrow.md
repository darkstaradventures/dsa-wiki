The Harrows were a family who perished during the collapse of [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]]. The crew discovered their remains and belongings while exploring [[The Glendale|the Glendale]] Hotel.

Michael Harrow, who had worked in the financial sector before Wafisa's fall, was found dead slumped over a writing desk in a locked room on the hotel's fourth floor. He left behind recordings, letters, a laptop, a USB recording device, and clues pointing to a hidden machine and a family recipe book. His final recording warned any who found it: *"If you find this, don't stay here. Don't wait. Don't hunker down. Don't try to wait it out. Leave this planet as soon as you can. There's nothing left here for people like us."*

Claire Harrow, his wife, was found deceased in the bed of the same hotel room, her long blonde hair stringy with time. Michael had left behind a letter and a folder of photos dedicated to her, and her garnet amulet was found on the bedside table.

Their child, Liam Harrow, is unaccounted for. His room, found at [[17 Millbrook Lane]], was decorated with children's toys and clown and dolphin motifs, and contained two decks of [[Lokipon Cards]]. His fate remains unknown.

Among the family's belongings, the crew recovered a pre-collapse luxury hover car, the [[The Aurora|Aurora LX-90]], found in [[Millbrook Storage]], a compact energy-cell handgun with an AI aiming system, the [[Harrow Family Cookbook]], and a set of seventeen octagonal coppery-bronze coins bearing the insignia of the [[Guardians of the Great Work]].
