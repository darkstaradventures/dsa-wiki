The Fork is an alien device built around a forked pylon of black metal, with energy running between its tines. In Ep 24 the Game Master calls it the Paracausal Bifurcation Array (PBA), and [[Rynn Kade]] says her corporation, [[Nexus Trading]], calls it the Ontic Fork. She says it allows communication across vast distances and the creation of new [[Virtual Intelligence|virtual intelligences]].

**Human-built forks.** At the truck stop at [[_Scrapjacks|Scrapjack's]], [[Void Cultists]] set up a two-pronged antenna that held open a rip in space between its prongs, with something tentacled moving inside (Ep 10, Ep 11). [[Haleyy]] destroyed it by punching it (Ep 12). In Ep 17 the Game Master describes it as "cobbled together" and human, unlike the alien pylon. At the [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] auction a Void Cultist ship fired an antenna that split into two prongs, and everyone on the landing pad went blank and stood at attention (Ep 21). Haleyy shot the fork off the ship's prow with the Scapegoat's rail gun, and those already converted stayed [[The Unhallowed|Unhallowed]] (Ep 22, Ep 23).

**The Sibylen pylon.** In a round room at the bottom of the alien exosite, a forked pylon stood at the center of a nautilus-shaped spiral ridge, with an impression between the tines matching one by the goo pool upstairs (Ep 17). Once the spiral was filled with nanofluid, [[Dr. Elara Voss]] activated it (Ep 18, Ep 19). A ten-foot cubic window opened between the tines and traveled across space to [[Prometheon Station]]'s gyroscope. It showed a blue light that split into diamonds, each a mass of black metal limbs with desires [[Felix Champlin]] and Voss could not parse. Haleyy screamed and collapsed unconscious (Ep 18). The Ep 21 recap says "something looked back." This exosite is called [[Exosite 34b]] on air.

**Ventos.** In [[Robert Ross]]'s flashback, a spire like a large tuning fork stood in a cavern on [[_Ventos|Ventos]]. Energy crackled between its tines, showed a planet with red clouds and a red sun, and struck "Haley" (the one-Y Haley of Robert's story), whose eyes turned green (Ep 19 flashback). Ep 24 narrates Haleyy's recovered memory as lying comatose in "this very room" while "this very alien fork was activated."

**Ep 24.** In Exosite 34b, a three-tined Fork stood with [[Meer Prust]], [[Sally Gaylen]], and [[Vera Corbin]] wired to it by human-made cables, and an orb hovered between the tines. Felix could not hold the orb. When Haleyy healed [[Thorne]], the Fork shut down and the three bodies died (Ep 24). Felix theorized that its overload sent energy through Haleyy (Ep 25). [[Dr. Iso]] later said the same thing happened to [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Froggy]] with very different results (Ep 28).

**Prometheon.** Haleyy said the void energy sparking off the gyroscope matched what came out of the forks (Ep 31). [[Sabien]] carried a pendant that was a shard of a fork, which Haleyy used to stop one Unhallowed with a command, and which flew out of her hand when pressed to Voss (Ep 32). In Ep 33 he said [[The Listeners]], hired by [[Virodyne Labs]], make the two-tined forks themselves, while three-tined and sometimes four-tined ones come from alien ruins. The three-tined kind was used to put human consciousness into an orb (Ep 33). The show does not call the gyroscope a fork.
