The Exo-Net is the sector's interplanetary information and communications network. Personal messages sent over it are called exo-mail. Setting material describes it as a network of automated data relays, and the show treats it as the normal way people look things up and write to each other across the sector.

Distance limits how useful it is. On [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]], the Exo-Net runs through a relay that connects to the inner system. Information not already cached locally can take days, weeks, or months to arrive, because nothing travels faster than light, and the GM described the Exo-Net that far out as almost useless (Ep 26). Sibylen's residents also share a local network of their own (Ep 26).

Access can be cut off or controlled. On [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]], [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Froggy]] told the crew that he and [[Vera Corbin]] had been writing to each other over the Exo-Net until "the Guardian" shut it down, up until just a few years earlier (Ep 14). Earlier, the GM said Wafisa had not had outside contact in 20 years (Ep 6). [[Nexus Trading]] has its own subnet of the Exo-Net, and an anonymous, senderless message arriving on it was described as something that should have been impossible (Ep 13 flashback).

Characters use the Exo-Net for research, correspondence, and tracking. [[Thorne]] bought 500 hours of Exo-Net time to hunt for [[William Sallerist]] (Ep 13 flashback). At the end of the [[_Scrapjacks|Scrapjacks]] arc, Thorne said he and [[Yana 7]] had swapped exo-mail so they could stay in touch (Ep 12). [[Dr. Elara Voss]], speaking as a [[Virtual Intelligence|virtual intelligence]], said she was traced after the gas main went off and she lost power and disconnected from the Exo-Net (Ep 8). In the alien vault she compared the ancient network to the Exo-Net and said it gave her the same feeling as connecting to it (Ep 15). Searches for Prometheon came back empty "exonet or no" (Ep 28). During the chase before the crew reached [[_Prometheon Station|Prometheon Station]], a voice from the pursuing ship said it held backups of the Exo-Net containing the notices and warrants that identified the crew (Ep 29).
