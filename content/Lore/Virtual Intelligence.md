A Virtual Intelligence (VI) is a human consciousness transferred into an alien orb. The Game Master spelled out the distinction in Ep 14: artificial intelligence is common, virtual intelligences "don't exist," and [[Dr. Elara Voss]] is "a straight up consciousness transfer into this alien artifact."

**Dr. Voss.** Voss told the crew in Ep 8 that she was a xenolinguist on a team that found an orb on [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]]. When the team introduced electrical current to it, an explosion killed them all, and she woke inside the orb. She said she was either a copy or a transferred consciousness, and that she had been a virtual intelligence for two years. The orb is a steel-blue sphere covered in alien glyphs that glow when she speaks (Ep 8). Ep 21 calls her "the first human virtual intelligence." [[Viktor Strake]] said [[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne]] had been trying to make one for a long time (Ep 21).

**What a VI does.** In the [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] exosite, the orb fit an indent beside the goo pool, and Voss found that a switch there required a VI and an organic being working together (Ep 15). In Ep 24, [[Rynn Kade]] said a VI is needed to operate the exosite and any other alien technology. After Voss wired herself into the Scapegoat's comms panel, some ship systems depended on an artificial or virtual intelligence to run them (Ep 27).

**Making new ones.** Rynn Kade said humans cannot make a VI alone, so [[Nexus Trading]] used an orb from "our masters" and the [[The Fork|Fork]], and that one human cannot survive the transfer, so three people are combined into one. In Ep 24 the three were [[Meer Prust]], [[Sally Gaylen]], and [[Vera Corbin]], all converted [[The Unhallowed|Unhallowed]] and wired to the Fork. She said the untethered could then control them and plug them into alien interfaces. [[Felix Champlin]] saw that the glyphs on this orb differed from Voss's. The three bodies died when the Fork shut down, and the orb stayed inert (Ep 24, Ep 25). Afterward Vera was spoken of as being inside an orb (Ep 28, Ep 31).

**Prometheon.** At [[Prometheon Station]], [[Petra and Ren]] said they had connected a VI to an array on the alien gyroscope. Petra said VI orbs usually hold an alien consciousness, and the gyroscope's transmissions could not be decoded because "our virtual intelligence is not compatible" (Ep 32, Ep 33). Felix moved the connection from Voss to the three-person orb, and a chorus on the comms said "We are the Concordant" (Ep 33). See [[The Concordant]].
