The Unhallowed are people who have been converted by the [[Void Cultists]] and left without free will. The Ep 24 recap describes them as "humans stripped of their free will." They appear in the same stretch of episodes as the [[The Fork|Fork]], which was used to convert them at the [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] auction.

**How people become Unhallowed.** At the truck stop at [[_Scrapjacks|Scrapjack's]], a cultist told captives that those who can be "untethered" join the cult through a ritual, and "those that cannot be untethered instead become unhallowed" and serve the void (Ep 10). Another cultist called an Unhallowed person "a husk" and "a tool" (Ep 11). At the auction on Sibylen, a Void Cultist ship's antenna fired, and everyone on the landing pad stopped, their eyes went blank, and they stood at attention. [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Froggy]] and [[Vera Corbin]] were among them (Ep 21). Destroying that antenna did not reverse the change (Ep 22, Ep 23). People who had stayed inside the auction tent were killed instead (Ep 23).

**Untethered and Unhallowed.** "Untethered" is the term the cultists use for people they can bring into the cult, such as [[Haleyy]], whom a cultist called "touched the void already" (Ep 10). In Ep 24 the Hierophant [[Kethrix]] examined [[Thorne]] and called him a "candidate," and called Haleyy "untethered." [[Rynn Kade]] told Thorne the cultists would untether him rather than make him Unhallowed, and said "we control them" when Thorne asked whether an Unhallowed person could come back (Ep 24).

**Behavior.** In [[Exosite 34b]] the Unhallowed carried boxes and packed equipment, and Rynn Kade ordered them around (Ep 23, Ep 24). Froggy wept while he worked and did not respond to the crew (Ep 23). Telling him to stop had no effect (Ep 23).

**Haleyy's command.** Haleyy found she could command the Unhallowed with her voice. Froggy picked up a box and went to sit in a corner when she told him to (Ep 23), and she sent other Unhallowed to sit with him (Ep 24). By Ep 30 Froggy was acting on his own again, and the question came up whether time and distance from the Fork had cured him.

**Prometheon.** On [[Prometheon Station]], station workers in company and work uniforms, with a white pallor and blue-tinged eyes, walked toward the crew in groups, and Haleyy identified them as Unhallowed (Ep 31). They moved slowly and then ran, clawing and gibbering. Haleyy's shouted command made only two of them stumble (Ep 31), and holding a shard of a fork let her stop one more (Ep 32). [[Sabien]] said the [[The Listeners|Listeners]] had controlled them through "concentration and power of will," but that "they no longer listen to us. They listen to the gyroscope" (Ep 33). Haleyy's order to run away failed, and the horde stopped and dispersed when [[The Concordant]] spoke over the comms (Ep 33).
