An ownership module is a proprietary module found in many machines across the sector, from repair bots to starships, that identifies who owns a given piece of technology (Ep 22, Ep 25). Modules are pressed once, like records, and cannot be reprogrammed, so the only way to change ownership is to buy a new blank one and scribe it (Ep 22). Unless a buyer works for a corporation, there is no legitimate way to purchase one (Ep 25), so they are sold on the black market (Ep 22).

In Ep 21, [[Viktor Strake]] locked the crew out of [[The Scapegoat]] with his codes, sealing them inside the ship and revoking [[Felix Champlin]]'s access to its systems. Felix traced the lockout to the ship's ownership module, which was digitally signed by [[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne]], and the GM explained that Strake took over the ship with the admin key (Ep 22). Felix later said [[Omnitek Holdings]] had been embedded in the ship's core systems through the module and could have used that to track the crew (Ep 26). The crew could restore their security systems but could not fly or open the doors, and the only fix was to buy a replacement and install it (Ep 22, Ep 23).

They went to [[_The Spigot|The Spigot]] to find one (Ep 25). [[Dr. Iso]] said she did not carry ownership modules herself because they were too hot to handle, and led the crew to [[Peter Haslow]], who scribes and installs them for repair bots. He told them there was no legitimate way to get one, and [[Thorne]] later reported that Haslow had given him specific details of where to look (Ep 25). The plan was to intercept a cargo ship due at the helium docks at 3 a.m. carrying blank ownership modules (Ep 26, Ep 27). During the raid on [[Helium Docks]], Thorne found a box of loose ownership module chips, and the crew escaped under drone attack and an ambush on the road back (Ep 27). Felix installed a new module in the Scapegoat. Dr. [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]] had permanently wired herself into the ship's communications panel and was no longer aboard, so [[Buddy AI]] was switched on to replace her (Ep 27).

The crew kept spare modules. Felix said the shuttle *Life is Unfair* had been re-registered with the extra ones so it could not be traced (Ep 28). At [[Causeway 3]], a Virodyne routing station, the Scapegoat's module was flagged and access was denied, and a station voice asked why it showed as non-Virodyne and registered to Scapegoat LLC. Thorne answered that borrowing another vessel had caused a mix-up with the modules (Ep 28).
