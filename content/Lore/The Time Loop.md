The time loop is a repeating cycle that trapped the crew of the Scapegoat on [[Prometheon Station]] in Ep 31 to Ep 33.

**Entry.** [[The Scapegoat]] dropped out of its spike drive beside the station's five-ring gyroscope, with three rings turning and two locked and sparking where they met, and a dozen [[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne]] warships hanging motionless nearby (Ep 31). The crew was overrun in a small room by [[The Unhallowed]], drones, and gas-masked Listeners. After [[Thorne]]'s data pad played the deposit alert "Redshift Revelry," he raised his rifle, and the whole crew found themselves back on the Scapegoat's bridge at arrival, remembering the battle. [[Felix Champlin]] called it a time loop (Ep 31).

**How it resets.** In Ep 32 the crew died repeatedly (shot, torn apart by a mob, killed by grenades, burned by acid) and each time woke on the bridge uninjured, with [[Haleyy]] vomiting each time. They kept their memories and used them to move faster, such as knowing the route to the lab and the CEO's office. By Ep 33 Thorne believed the alert sound came before every reset.

**Who does not reset.** [[Dr. Iso]] did not reset with the crew and spent about an hour alone on the station after they died (Ep 32). The lab scientists [[Petra and Ren]] were melted by gas at the same time every loop, and Petra counted 21 loops (Ep 32). [[Sabien]] and [[Viktor Strake]] also spoke of earlier loops (Ep 32, Ep 33).

**Cause.** Petra said the scientists had connected a [[Virtual Intelligence]] to an array on the alien gyroscope, that it "isn't the right fit," and that this was probably why the loop was happening. [[Victor Saren]] had ordered the experiment to proceed (Ep 32).

**Notable loops.** In Ep 32 Felix used the CEO's terminal to turn on the lab gas and killed Petra and Ren, then unplugged [[Dr. Elara Voss]] against her warning. The station reset. By Ep 33 the gyroscope was close enough to pull the station apart, and the [[The Listeners|Listeners]] had lost control of the Unhallowed.

**End.** Ren warned that the gyroscope's energy would reach a second orb and collapse spacetime in the sector. Felix moved the connection from Voss to the second orb, which carried three human consciousnesses, and a chorus identifying itself as [[The Concordant]] spoke over the comms. The horde froze and dispersed (Ep 33). Thorne killed Strake by shooting out his boots so that he was pulled to the gyroscope, and the deposit alert played again without a reset (Ep 33).

**Aftermath.** About a month later, Thorne's log said Felix had recovered Voss, who was quieter than before (Ep 33).
