The Red Rice Wars were a war fought on the jungle planet [[_Ventos|Ventos]]. The show describes the conflict mainly through Robert's account of his time there (Ep 18, Ep 19) and the flashback that goes with it.

According to the Ep 19 flashback narration, Ventos was a colony in turmoil. A rice shortage left the people starving, while the wealthy held what food stores remained. Rebellion grew into a full-blown war, and the narration says the elite were losing it. The shortage was blamed on an invasive insect from off-world, which swarmed through the jungle as clouds of small black dots and consumed everything in sight. The side in power is called the oligarchy, and its forces are called elites. The rebels fought them.

[[Robert Ross]] says he was on Ventos with his squad at "the tail end" of the war, a few years before the crew's time at the [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] exosite, and recalls the heat, humidity, and swarms of bugs (Ep 18, Ep 19). His squad, made up of clones of himself, was sent to extract his commander, Haley with one Y (the woman who became [[Haleyy]]), who was held captive in a cave outpost of the elites. See [[Strike Force 6]]. The extraction went badly: Haley was struck by energy from an alien structure in the caves, the squad's helicopter was shot down by an anti-air mech while a battle between rebels and elites raged nearby, and Haley died (Ep 19 flashback).

In Ep 8, [[Dr. Elara Voss|Dr. Voss]] showed the crew footage and documents about the squad's final mission. Voss said Robert had thought he was sent to liberate a colony and assist in the war. The documents claimed that [[Omnitek Holdings]] and [[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne]] were complicit in Haley's death, and that the mission was a test of whether to continue the kind of experiments and training that produced Robert. Robert questioned whether the documents were genuine.

Later mentions:
- At the auction, [[Thorne]] reported overhearing a Virodyne representative and a NovaCrypt representative say something about "Aussie One and the Red Rice War" (Ep 21).
- Robert asked [[Dr. Iso]] whether she remembered the Red Rice Wars, and she answered that she practically invented them (Ep 25).
- Iso asked Haleyy whether they had fought "the same red rice wars," and the Ep 27 narration says Haleyy had flashbacks to a similar situation with Iso during the war (Ep 27).
- Robert played a track called "Red Rice Riot" on the [[The Aurora|Aurora]]'s audio system. The GM described it as this universe's "Fortunate Son," and Thorne remarked that Robert had been on the other side of it (Ep 27).

The show does not say how the war ended.
