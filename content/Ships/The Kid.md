The Kid is a small Omnitek shuttle the crew captured at The Spigot spaceport in Episode 18. Built to transport two people on long trips (or squeeze in four on short ones), it can be stored inside [[The Scapegoat]]'s cargo hold, taking up about an eighth of the total space, though it cannot be launched mid-flight from a frigate.

Formerly registered under Omnitek as *Life Is Unfair*, the crew renamed it after capture; its ownership module is still locked to Omnitek and needs replacing.

When the crew found it, the shuttle held a [[Robert Ross]] clone claiming to be alone, though evidence suggested a second occupant had been aboard. Orders found on the shuttle instructed its crew to follow, then eliminate, the PCs. Loot recovered aboard included several thousand credits, an encrypted datapad, a spike thrower, and a set of security armor.
