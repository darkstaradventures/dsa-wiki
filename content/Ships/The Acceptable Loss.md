The Acceptable Loss is [[Viktor Strake]]'s personal ship, first encountered in Episode 7. Triangular, with three bird-like landing legs, it has the silhouette of a stealth jet. It carries one or two clone bodies in stasis at all times, kept in reserve for Strake's use.
