The Acceptable Loss is [[Viktor Strake]]'s personal ship, first encountered in Episode 7. Triangular, with three bird-like landing legs, it has the silhouette of a stealth jet. It carries one or two clone bodies in stasis at all times, kept in reserve for Strake's use.

It was docked against [[Prometheon Station]]'s flank throughout the time loop, anchored to the station itself. On the station's final, unraveling loop (Ep 33), [[Robert Ross|Robert]] fired the Scapegoat's sand chucker into it before it could be pulled apart by the collapsing gyroscope. Everything but a single small anchored chunk was torn free and lost to the void; what little remained kept tumbling past as the crew's fight inside the station played out, eventually swallowed by the gyroscope along with the rest of the wreckage.
