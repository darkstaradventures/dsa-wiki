The Thalia is a two-person hauler, originally part of the Draxus Goods fleet and now running the old Draxus trade route under [[Nexus Trading]] control, crewed by Callum Vex and Harlan Rigg. Its route runs from [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]] to [[_Polyphn|Polyphn]] to Zephryss III, carrying alien artifacts recovered from Blackharbor and Wafisa to be processed and sold. It's currently parked in the auxiliary lot of the Rust Yards in [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]].

The ship is named after Thalia Draxus — [[Thorne]]'s mother, and the woman who handed Draxus Goods over to Nexus Trading. The ship predates that takeover; only the name stuck.
