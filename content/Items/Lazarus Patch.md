A Lazarus patch is an emergency medical item applied to a wounded person to stop bleeding and stabilize them. The transcripts also call it a Lazarus pack. It is carried in medkits and survival kits (Ep 7, Ep 31), and the crew treats it as a standard piece of field medical gear.

Applying one takes a skill check, and the check becomes harder the longer the patient has gone untreated: the GM explained a penalty of minus one for each round after the first since the injury (Ep 7).

Notable uses:
- In Ep 3, [[Robert Ross]] ran to [[Haleyy]] after a fight and applied Lazarus packs one after another until he had used his full supply. She came back to consciousness. The Ep 4 recap says Haleyy and [[Thorne]] were both mortally wounded and brought back by medical care and Lazarus patches.
- In Ep 7, Haleyy offered a patch from a survival kit to [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Froggy]], who had been shot. Robert applied it three rounds after the shot, rolled well enough to overcome the penalty, and Froggy sighed and fell unconscious. His bleeding slowed afterward.
- In Ep 19, Robert's flashback has him use a Lazarus patch on his commander, Haley with one Y, after the helicopter crash on [[_Ventos|Ventos]]. He worried he had applied it wrong. She stirred briefly, then had no pulse.
- In Ep 27, Thorne found 15 Lazarus patches, a bioscanner, and five medkits in a crate of [[Omnitek Holdings|Omnitek]] pharmaceuticals at the [[Helium Docks]].
- In Ep 31, Thorne took a patch from his medkit and applied it to [[Felix Champlin]] after a Listener's laser bolt knocked him down. Felix appeared to be breathing afterward.
- In Ep 32, after the crew found themselves back aboard the Scapegoat, Felix said that a Lazarus patch is good "but not that good" when his gunshot wound was simply gone.
