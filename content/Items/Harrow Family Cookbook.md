The Harrow Family Cookbook is a red notebook containing recipes spanning three generations of the Harrow family, found at 17 Millbrook Lane on [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]]. It has roughly 100 blank pages in the back. The book belonged to Michael and Claire Harrow, whose bodies were found at the Glendale Hotel.

[[Haleyy]] has carried the cookbook with her ever since, the same way the crew treasures [[The Aurora]] — a small, human touchstone amid the rest of their travels.
