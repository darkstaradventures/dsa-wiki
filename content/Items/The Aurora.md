The Aurora (LX-90) is a plutonium-powered hover car, essentially a nuclear-powered two-door sports car, discovered behind a false wall in storage bay 17 at Millbrook Storage on [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]], following instructions left behind by Michael Harrow at the Glendale Hotel. Despite years of abandonment, it's still fully operational thanks to its nuclear power source.

The car held personal effects from the Harrow family, including a photograph and a leather jacket that [[Thorne]] later claimed for himself. It's now stored in [[The Scapegoat]]'s cargo hold, sharing space with Reginald "Froggy" Vance's living quarters.

The Aurora also appeared in one of [[Haleyy]]'s dreams, in which her mother was seated in a crashed version of the car.
