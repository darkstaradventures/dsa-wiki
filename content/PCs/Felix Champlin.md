Felix Champlin is the hacker and communications specialist aboard [[The Scapegoat]], played by Scott. Born in 2649 on New Lucerne, Felix has a prosthetic right leg (from the hip down) that causes him chronic pain, which he sometimes manages with drugs. He prefers machines to people, works from a foldout three-screen "data slab," and is driven by one overriding goal: rescuing his brother.

## Backstory

Felix's younger brother, Petey Champlin, was a journalist and field medic working undercover for Blackout Press inside [[Omnitek Holdings]], investigating the corporation's prisoner conversion program under the pen name "Dox Ryker." Felix, Petey, and two accomplices broke into Omnitek's orbital data vault above [[_Polyphn|Polyphn]] and discovered Operation Red Wing: Omnitek's scheme to buy prisoner contracts and digitize the prisoners' consciousness as AI test subjects, killing the bodies in the process. Petey was killed during the heist by a device that copies consciousness and destroys the body; his digitized mind remains trapped in Omnitek's servers, running through nightmare simulations.

## Powers and Growth

During the crew's exploration of an alien digital space, Felix received knowledge directly from Dr. Elara Voss, gaining a bonus to his technical skill that applies specifically to alien-tech interfaces. He can manipulate alien gel-mold interfaces with his programming skill, and made a working copy of [[Buddy AI]]'s source code and personality after hacking it at Scrapjacks. That copy has since helped run the Scapegoat's systems.

## Gear

Felix carries a stun baton, a knife, and a Stauser pistol, and wears Secure Clothing for armor. His readied gear includes his dataslab, a line shunt, and a metatool.

## Character Spotlights

Free Patreon bonus content exploring Felix's early life covers his childhood discovery of a gift for hardware at a coastal electronics dump, and a teenage repair of his neighborhood's water reclaimer that shaped his belief that systems fail because people stop asking the right questions.
