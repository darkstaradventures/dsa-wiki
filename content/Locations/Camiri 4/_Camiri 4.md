Camiri 4 is a highly populated space station in the Schroeter system, orbiting the gas giant [[Camiri]]. It is ruled by martial law and carved up into sections controlled by various gangs, mafias, and policed communities. Once a hub for research and development, it has spent the past twenty years becoming a haven for black market drugs and illicit activity.

The station's corridors are dirty and grimy, organized by numbered docking bay sections, with the air smelling of rust and old fuel and malfunctioning lights flickering overhead. A simulated day/night cycle dims the lights on a timer and warms their color toward evening. There is no governing authority — pure anarchy rules the station.

In its prime, Camiri 4 was a bustling hub of activity, the launching-off point for scientific and industrial ventures headed into the southern and southeastern outer reaches of civilized space. Scientists and adventurers used it as a base for exploring new worlds, and [[Omnitek Holdings]] and other corporations kept a foothold here. About twenty years ago, a rebellion on the station left many dead and pushed all corporate interests out — it was simply too dangerous to stay. The station became overrun by independent factions, like the [[Drift Rats]], who carved out their own territory, and the corporations decided it wasn't worth the trouble of retaking.

The station is located on the "south side" of [[Polyphn]], off to the east — the opposite direction from [[Sibylen]] in the outer reaches of civilized space.

Sub-locations include the [[Drift Rats]]' territory in Docking Bays 50-80, the [[Neon Fangs Territory|Neon Fangs' territory]] in Docking Bays 22-43, [[Roger's Billiards]] within Neon Fangs territory, and the [[Gas Mining Front]], a fake Omnitek recruiting office and the only Omnitek presence on the station.

The crew was recruited here via an Omnitek pre-recorded briefing (Ep 1). They had to negotiate with the [[Drift Rats]] to reach their ship, hacking the [[Neon Fangs]]' network as payment (Ep 2), and named the ship [[The Scapegoat]]. Their departure was contested by a space battle with two strike fighters (Ep 2).
