The Gas Mining Front is a fake recruiting office for a gas mining company on [[Camiri 4]], the only [[Omnitek Holdings]] location on the station. It served as a cover for Omnitek's operations, and it's where the crew received their initial mission briefing via a pre-recorded message (Ep 1). The briefing directed them to pick up [[Dr. Elara Voss]] on [[Wafisa]] and investigate a mysterious signal.
