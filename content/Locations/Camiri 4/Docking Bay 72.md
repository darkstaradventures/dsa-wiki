Docking Bay 72 is a docking bay on [[_Camiri 4|Camiri 4]] where a ship was waiting for the crew. Ep 8 reveals it was a practically derelict [[Omnitek Holdings|Omnitek]] merchant class ship that Dr. [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]] arranged for them. The mission briefing in Ep 1 says a free merchant class spaceship had been purchased and awaited them in Docking Bay 72. The ship had not flown in close to a decade, was on no known registration, and came with a false ship identification loaded on a data chip. This is the ship the crew later flies as [[The Scapegoat]].

The bay sits in the section of docking bays numbered 50 through 80, behind a barricade held by the Drift Rats. See [[Drift Rats Territory]]. Corridors in that area show scorch marks and old blood from an earlier war on the station (Ep 1). The gang's leader, [[Torik Rustclaw Sarr|Rustclaw]], told the crew that the [[Drift Rats]] had controlled these bays for years but had been unable to open Docking Bay 72, and he did not know what was inside it (Ep 1). He agreed to let the crew in only after they carried out a job for the gang.

When the crew returned in Ep 2, the bay's door was shut. Along its seam were scorch marks, bent metal, pry marks, and blood, with graffiti sprayed across the front, and a data pad sat beside it. When [[Felix Champlin]] put the SD card from the mission envelope into the pad, the door sprang open on well-oiled hinges. Inside was the merchant ship, which opens by a long rear ramp. Rustclaw then tried to claim the ship and attacked Felix, and the crew killed him and left the bay with the ship (Ep 2).
