Drift Rats Territory occupies Docking Bays 50-80 on [[Camiri 4]], held by the [[Drift Rats]] gang. The entrance is barricaded, with scorch marks and dried blood on the walls from a past battle. The barricade, about ten feet tall, is built from stacked storage bins and metal sheeting, watched by two armed guards on a ledge behind it, wearing hacked-together armor painted bright green and yellow, with a red rat emblem on the shoulder.

The crew had to negotiate passage through here to reach [[The Scapegoat]] (Ep 1-2), which had been parked in Docking Bay 72 within this territory for years. [[Torik Rustclaw Sarr|Rustclaw]], the gang's leader, refused to let them through until they hacked the [[Neon Fangs]]' network in exchange for passage.
