Drift Rats Territory occupies Docking Bays 50-80 on [[Camiri 4]], held by the [[Drift Rats]] gang. It has a single point of entry, barricaded and guarded at all times, with scorch marks lining the walls. The barricades are built from stacked storage bins and metal sheeting, watched by two armed guards in hacked-together armor painted bright green and yellow and decorated with the gang's red rat emblem.

Inside, the territory is mostly large and empty docking bays, though a few strike fighters sit ready for use. It functions as a clubhouse for the gang, who commit crimes elsewhere and retreat here to stay safe — about a dozen Drift Rats are present at any given time.

The crew had to negotiate passage through here to reach [[The Scapegoat]] (Ep 1-2). Gang leader [[Torik Rustclaw Sarr]] offered them passage in exchange for hacking the [[Neon Fangs]]' network. The ship was stored in this territory before the crew claimed it.
