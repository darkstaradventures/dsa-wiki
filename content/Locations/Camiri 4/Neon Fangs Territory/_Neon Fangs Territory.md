Neon Fangs Territory occupies docking bays on [[Camiri 4]] running roughly from Docking Bay 20 up into the low forties, controlled by the [[Neon Fangs]] gang. It's a long, wide, open corridor patrolled by gang members, functioning essentially as a giant open-air market where people from all over the station come to trade. Most of the smaller docking bays here have been converted into businesses, including [[Roger's Billiards]], a pool hall owned by [[Roger Neronta]].

The crew passed through here during their escape from Camiri 4 (Ep 1-2). [[Felix Champlin|Felix]] hacked the Neon Fangs' communication network and plastered [[Drift Rats]] insignia across every screen with the message: "Better a Rat in the Drift than a King in a Cage."
