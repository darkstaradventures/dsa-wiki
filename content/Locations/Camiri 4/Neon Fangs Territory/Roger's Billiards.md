Roger's Billiards is a pool hall in [[Neon Fangs Territory]] on [[Camiri 4]], owned by [[Neon Fangs]] guard [[Roger Neronta|Roger]] and run day-to-day by his wife (who never gave her name) and eldest son, Axel, since Roger's guard duties leave him little time to work it himself. It has pool tables, a bar, and an old-fashioned digital jukebox stocked mostly with musicians from several generations back. The space is cluttered but clean, decorated with paintings, beaded curtains, badges, and stickers. A back storage area (holding food and drink stock) is accessible by hand-wave sensor and connects to the bathrooms, and a keypad-locked door leads to a room with a computer terminal and a security camera on a swivel mount.
