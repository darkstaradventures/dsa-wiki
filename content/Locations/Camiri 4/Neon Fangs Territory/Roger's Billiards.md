Roger's Billiards is a pool hall in [[Neon Fangs Territory]] on [[Camiri 4]], owned by [[Neon Fangs]] guard [[Roger Neronta]] and run day-to-day by his wife Cecelia and son Axle. It has five pool tables with room for around fifty people, a long metal bar with no front paneling, and an old-world jukebox modified to play hits from the station's notable artists. The space is cluttered but clean, decorated with paintings, beaded curtains, badges, and stickers. A storage room in back is accessible by hand-wave sensor and leads to the bathrooms, and a keypad-locked door connects to an adjacent room with a computer terminal and security camera.
