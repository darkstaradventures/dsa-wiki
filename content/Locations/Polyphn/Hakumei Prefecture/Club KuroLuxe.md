Club KuroLuxe is a nightclub in [[Hakumei Prefecture]] on [[Polyphn]]. Neon light spills from its entrance in blues, reds, and purples, flickering through the alley like a broken memory, bass thumping through the walls as a nearby vent hisses steam.

[[Thorne]] and [[Kano Rix|Kano]] met [[Mara Vex]] and [[Brick]] in the alley behind the club after receiving an anonymous message: "Sallerist lives. He's been erased. Meet in the alley behind KuroLuxe. Midnight. Bring muscle." Two Nexus goons ambushed them there, and Mara and Brick arrived in a car, launching a high-speed chase (Ep 13).
