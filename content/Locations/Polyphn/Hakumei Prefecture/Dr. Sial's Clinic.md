Dr. Sial's Clinic is a ripperdoc clinic deep in [[Polyphn]]'s underground, accessible through service tunnels and forgotten corridors beneath [[Hakumei Prefecture]]. The air is stale and heavy with ozone; a rusted door marked with a faded red cross leads inside. The clinic is cramped and cluttered with cybernetic parts and flickering monitors, surgical arms hanging overhead like dormant insects.

After the gravcar chase, [[Mara Vex]] led the group here to lay low and regroup. Dr. Sial, an old ally of Mara's, patched up the group and ran diagnostics on her bio-mods, showing that he could remotely see through her telescopic eye — the clue that revealed he had secretly been reporting to [[Nexus Trading]] all along (Ep 12).
