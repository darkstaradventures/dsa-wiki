Dr. Sial's Clinic is a ripperdoc clinic deep in [[Polyphn]]'s underground, reached through service tunnels and forgotten corridors where the air turns stale and heavy with ozone, behind a rusted door marked with a faded red cross. The clinic is cramped and cluttered with cybernetic parts and flickering monitors, surgical arms hanging overhead like dormant insects.

After the car chase, [[Mara Vex]] led the group here to lay low and regroup. Dr. Sial, an old ally of Mara's, patched up the group and ran diagnostics on her bio-mods (including a telescopic eye implant), seeing through her eye in the process. <!-- REVIEW: wiki previously claimed this scene revealed Dr. Sial was secretly reporting to Nexus -- not found in transcript, removed. -->
