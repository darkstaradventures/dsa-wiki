The Relay Tower is a skeletal antenna tower looming against the haze of [[Polyphn]]'s upper sprawl in [[Hakumei Prefecture]] — an ancient, rust-streaked structure with exposed steel lower levels, forgotten scaffolds, and open-air platforms bolted onto the frame. Cables whip and hum in the wind, hazard lights flicker through the gloom, and faded Nexus Corp logos cling to the metal, half-eaten by rust. It's one of many automated data relays that funnel Nexus Corp communications, each keeping a rolling seven-day cache of every transmission before permanent erasure.

The relay nearest the [[Bloc 6]] crash site was taken offline for "repairs" hours after the maglev derailment and never came back online — its cache contained the command that crashed the maglev, proving William Sallerist had faked his death. [[Thorne]] climbed the tower while [[Mara Vex]], [[Brick]], and Kano Rix fought off Nexus forces below. At the top, Thorne encountered his mother, Thalia Draxus, who revealed that she herself had framed him, not Sallerist alone. A Nexus skimmer and van attacked during the data extraction; Brick and Kano were killed in the fight, and Mara was taken (Ep 12).
