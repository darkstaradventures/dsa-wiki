The Relay Tower is a skeletal antenna tower, fenced in chain link, on the outskirts of [[Hakumei Prefecture]] on [[Polyphn]], a rust-streaked structure roughly a hundred and fifty feet tall with a control platform lattice-worked with stairways. It's one of many automated data relays that carry Nexus Corp communications, each keeping a rolling seven-day cache of every transmission before permanent erasure.

The relay nearest the [[Bloc 6]] crash site was taken offline for repairs hours after the crash and never came back online. [[Mara Vex]] believed its cache still held the command that proved William Sallerist's death, and the crash, had been staged. [[Thorne]] climbed the tower while Mara, [[Brick]], and [[Kano Rix|Kano]] held off Nexus forces below. At the top, Thorne encountered his mother, who revealed that she herself, not Sallerist, had framed him. A Nexus skimmer and van attacked during the data extraction; Brick and Kano were killed in the fight, and Mara was taken (Ep 13).
