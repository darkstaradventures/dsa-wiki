Hakumei Prefecture is one of many prefectures on the ultra-urbanized surface of [[Polyphn]], marked by rain-slicked metal walkways, plasteel grates, and neon-drenched alleys. The air smells of burnt synth oil and soy noodles, with sub-orbital skyways humming overhead.

It's home to [[Nexus Trading]] headquarters and the site of [[Thorne]]'s Episode 12 flashback adventure. Notable features include [[Club KuroLuxe]], a nightclub used as an alley meeting point; [[Dr. Sial's Clinic]], a ripperdoc clinic in the underground; [[The Relay Tower]], a decommissioned Nexus data relay antenna; [[Bloc 6]], a residential district destroyed in a staged maglev crash; an Omnitek barracks used for worker housing; and a retro-café where Thorne first researched William Sallerist on his datapad.

[[Thorne]] roamed Hakumei tracking down William Sallerist, meeting [[Mara Vex]] in the alley behind Club KuroLuxe, surviving a gravcar chase through the streets, retreating to Dr. Sial's Clinic in the underground, and joining an assault on The Relay Tower by Nexus forces, where his mother Thalia Draxus revealed the truth about Sallerist's betrayal (Ep 12).
