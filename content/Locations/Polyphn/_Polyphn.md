Polyphn is a night world in the Lebima system, with a hyper-urbanized surface, a patchwork of civilization lit by neon streaks, with suborbital skyways humming overhead. Buildings cover most of the land mass, and the city extends many levels below the surface as well, where service tunnels and forgotten corridors house unhoused populations that the police won't enter. It seems to always be raining. Outside the cities, the ground is mostly wasteland and scrub grass, with the occasional lone tree.

Polyphn is home to [[Omnitek Holdings]]' headquarters. [[Nexus Trading]] also holds territory here, including [[Hakumei Prefecture]].

Surface locations include [[Hakumei Prefecture]], with its [[Club KuroLuxe|nightclub]], [[Dr. Sial's Clinic|ripperdoc clinic]], and [[The Relay Tower]], as well as [[Bloc 6]], a residential district destroyed in a staged transport crash. In orbit sits [[Omnitek Holdings]]' [[The Orbital Data Vault]].

[[Felix Champlin|Felix]]'s data vault heist took place here (Ep 9 flashback), and [[Thorne]]'s neo-noir backstory adventure across Hakumei Prefecture with [[Mara Vex]], [[Brick]], and [[Kano Rix|Kano]] unfolded here as well (Ep 13 flashback).
