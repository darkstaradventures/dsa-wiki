Polyphn is a night world in the Lebima system with a hyper-urbanized surface, a patchwork of civilization lit by neon streaks, with suborbital skyways humming overhead. Buildings cover most of the land mass and extend dozens of levels below the surface.

Polyphn orbits its sun at the extreme far reaches of the gravity well, so little light reaches it that daylight barely registers, and a dense layer of industrial smog filters out whatever UV might otherwise get through. The planet runs entirely on artificial light: streetlights, neon signs, and building illumination dominate the visual landscape at all times, and it seems to always be raining. Deep underground, service tunnels and forgotten corridors house unhoused populations that the police won't enter.

Polyphn is home to [[Omnitek Holdings]] headquarters and the majority of its workforce, roughly 5,000 workers, mostly laborers mining deep below the surface. Dozens of smaller stations, satellites, and orbital platforms crowd the space around the planet.

The surface is divided among major corporations, each owning country-sized regions. [[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne Laboratories]] owns roughly half the planet; within its territory, [[Omnitek Holdings]] controls about a third, and [[Nexus Trading]] owns a state-sized region that includes [[Hakumei Prefecture]]. Neighborhoods within each section are organized into numbered "Blocs," with block numbers repeating between sections. Other corporations hold smaller territories elsewhere. No nature is visible anywhere, only dirt, cement, and twisted steel grates, with acidic rain crashing down constantly in some districts.

Surface locations include [[Hakumei Prefecture]], with its [[Club KuroLuxe|nightclub]], [[Dr. Sial's Clinic|ripperdoc clinic]], and [[The Relay Tower]], as well as [[Bloc 6]], a residential district destroyed in a staged maglev crash. In orbit sit the public Polyphn Station, [[Omnitek Holdings]]' [[The Orbital Data Vault]], a Novacrypt facility called CySec Station, and a Virodyne research station called Lab-01.

[[Felix Champlin|Felix]]'s data vault heist took place here (Ep 9 flashback), and [[Thorne]]'s neo-noir backstory adventure across Hakumei Prefecture with [[Mara Vex]], [[Brick]], and [[Kano Rix]] unfolded here as well (Ep 12 flashback).
