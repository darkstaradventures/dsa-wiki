Ventos is a planet in the Thraxis system with inhospitable jungles, extremely hot and humid, and it serves as the system's breadbasket despite the harsh terrain. [[Haleyy]] grew up on [[Vigrid]], another planet in the same system.

Ventos was the site of the Red Rice Wars, a famine-driven war triggered by an invasive offworld insect that destroyed most food production. Rice was resistant, making it the staple crop, but there wasn't enough to go around. The government, a so-called meritocracy predicated on "good ideas" but in reality an oligarchy of ancestral privilege, hoarded food stores. A rebellion ignited but was ultimately crushed with [[Omnitek Holdings]] military backing, and the oligarchy remains in power.

Dense jungle on Ventos hides a cave system containing an alien structure holding a three-tined fork, the same type of alien artifact found at the [[Sibylen]] exosite. "Haley" (with one Y), before she became [[Haleyy]], was stationed here on loan from Omnitek to defend the oligarchy, defected to help the rebels, and was captured. A squadron was sent to extract her from the oligarchy's elite captivity in the cave system; inside, the alien fork struck her with energy, her eyes turning green and hair singeing. The squad escaped on stolen motorcycles through the jungle, but an anti-air mech shot down their extraction helicopter, killing several squadmates. Haley died from her crash injuries and was later revived in the morgue with alien-enhanced healing, waking with amnesia as "Haleyy" (Ep 19 flashback).
