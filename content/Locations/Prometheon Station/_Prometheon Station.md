Prometheon Station is a facility tied to [[Virodyne Labs]], first glimpsed by the crew through a portal opened by the Paracausal Bifurcation Array (the Fork) at [[Exosite 34b]] on [[Sibylen]]. When [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]] activated the device, a window opened across space showing a massive structure: five interlocking rings arranged like a celestial gyroscope, with a long rod through its center. [[Robert Ross|Robert]] recognized it on sight and called out its name, though he did not explain how he knew it. The window then showed a distant blue light that multiplied into diamond shapes as it drew near. [[Haleyy]] screamed and collapsed unconscious as the vision hit (Ep 18).

The station's deeper significance, and how Robert knew its name, went unexplained for many episodes afterward. It resurfaced concretely when the crew infiltrated the secret Virodyne routing station [[Causeway 3]]: transit logs there confirmed that [[Viktor Strake|Strake]]'s ship, the Acceptable Loss, had delivered the captured Voss to Prometheon Station (Ep 28), the first hard confirmation of her location since her capture. A deeper layer of the same records showed that the [[Omnitek Holdings|Omnitek]] data vault, carrying [[Petey Champlin|Petey]]'s digitized consciousness, had also been routed toward Prometheon Station years earlier and never returned. The crew obtained the drill path to Prometheon Station from Causeway 3's database before fleeing under pursuit (Ep 29).

As of the crew's escape from Causeway 3, Prometheon Station remains unreached: a destination now confirmed, but not yet explored.
