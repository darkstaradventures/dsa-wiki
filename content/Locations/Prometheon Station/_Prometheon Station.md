Prometheon Station is a facility tied to [[Virodyne Labs]], first glimpsed by the crew through a portal opened by the Paracausal Bifurcation Array (the Fork) at [[Exosite 34b]] on [[Sibylen]]. When [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]] activated the device, a window opened across space showing a massive structure: five interlocking rings arranged like a celestial gyroscope, with a long rod through its center. [[Robert Ross|Robert]] recognized it on sight and called out its name, though he did not explain how he knew it. The window then showed a distant blue light that multiplied into diamond shapes as it drew near. [[Haleyy]] screamed and collapsed unconscious as the vision hit (Ep 18).

The station's deeper significance, and how Robert knew its name, went unexplained for many episodes afterward. It resurfaced concretely when the crew infiltrated the secret Virodyne routing station [[Causeway 3]]: transit logs there confirmed that [[Viktor Strake|Strake]]'s ship, the Acceptable Loss, had delivered the captured Voss to Prometheon Station (Ep 28), the first hard confirmation of her location since her capture. A deeper layer of the same records showed that the [[Omnitek Holdings|Omnitek]] data vault, carrying [[Petey Champlin|Petey]]'s digitized consciousness, had also been routed toward Prometheon Station years earlier and never returned. The crew obtained the drill path to Prometheon Station from Causeway 3's database before fleeing under pursuit (Ep 29).

The crew finally arrived after an eighteen-day transit (Ep 31), dropping out to see the gyroscope up close for the first time: three of its five rings still turning, two locked dead, void energy sparking and raging where a moving ring passes a frozen one. Below it sits the station itself, with [[The Acceptable Loss]] docked against its flank and a dozen Virodyne warships hanging dead in formation further out. The station let the Scapegoat dock without requesting a single code, and the outer ring corridor beyond the airlock was eerily empty of people despite clear signs of recent activity: running environmentals, an unlocked terminal, a still-warm cup of coffee, motion-sensor lights.

A shipboard map obtained through a public data port showed two data centers, a laboratory wing, and several sections marked only "restricted." Exploring toward the security office, the crew fought off a pair of automated combat drones and picked up a mass-transit tram system to save time. They also picked up a tail: a masked group calling themselves [[The Listeners|the Listeners]], and behind them a horde of [[Unhallowed Stages|Unhallowed]] pouring out from a barricaded section of the station. Fleeing the horde, the crew crossed a catwalk over an open-air laboratory and spotted [[Viktor Strake]] watching them from a glassed-in control room, who taunted them over the local radio before letting them keep running.

The chase ended at a sealed lab holding [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]], wired to a pedestal, guarded by two researchers who tried to warn the crew before dying to a burst of noxious gas released into their own room. A pitched fight followed in the cramped space against drones, Listeners, and the encroaching Unhallowed horde, leaving [[Felix Champlin|Felix]] at 0 HP before a Lazarus patch from [[Thorne]] saved him. As the horde finally broke through the door, the scene reset without warning: the crew found themselves back on the Scapegoat's bridge, 45 minutes out from the station, with full memory of everything that had just happened. Prometheon Station turned out to be caught in, or generating, a time loop.

Reliving the same stretch of hours over and over with their memories intact (Ep 32), the crew slowly mapped the station and learned that the gas trap protecting Voss and her two researcher guards runs on a dead man's switch only the station's CEO can clear from his own office. The station turned out to be run day to day by [[The Listeners|the Listeners]], a faction of gas-masked [[Void Cultists]] led by [[Sabien]], with an army of shambling Unhallowed serving as their labor and security force; [[Viktor Strake]] was also present, trapped in the same loop and working an agenda of his own rather than the Listeners'. On the loop the crew finally pushed all the way into the CEO's office, they learned the gas is tied to that dead man's switch, found a photograph tying the CEO, [[Victor Saren|Saren]], directly to Strake, and recovered a data chip full of files on [[Project Red Wing]]. [[Felix Champlin|Felix]] forced the gas himself to buy time, killing the two trapped researchers for real, then disconnected Voss from the station's rig against her own warning just as the loop reset again.
