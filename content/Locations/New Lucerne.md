New Lucerne is the home world of [[Felix Champlin]], in the system catalogued as LR-7 (Low Resource 7). The published sector guide describes it as a resource-poor, heavy-industry world with a small, declining population. In the same system, Lucerne is the original colony, which failed. New Lucerne was the second attempt at settling the system.

Felix said he grew up on an industrial world, and named it as New Lucerne (Ep 22). The Game Master joked that drill sounds and police sirens were everywhere, "just in Felix's house."

Felix's prosthetic leg came from New Lucerne. In Episode 10 he told another character that "the military geniuses on New Lucerne" could not fit a good prosthetic, so they strapped on a metal leg that hurts him every day.

In Episode 28 the crew plotted a course from coordinates left by [[Dr. Elara Voss]]. The route ran through New Lucerne, which [[Thorne]] called Felix's "old stomping grounds," and Felix said they would have to pass through "blasted New Lucerne." Later in the episode [[Robert Ross]] asked how soon authorities from New Lucerne could intercept the ship, and Felix estimated hours to days.
