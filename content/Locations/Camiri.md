Camiri is an uninhabited gas giant in the Schroeter system. Over the years, four space stations were built in its orbit, numbered sequentially as they were constructed.

Camiri 1 was destroyed after its orbit decayed and it fell into the planet. Camiri 2 is a derelict currently in a decaying orbit, on the same trajectory toward destruction — extremely dangerous to approach. Camiri 3 is derelict and abandoned but sits in a stable orbit, making it safe to visit, though nobody is known to have done so. [[Camiri 4]] is the only active station, a gang-controlled anarchy and the starting location of the crew's campaign.
