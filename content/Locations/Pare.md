Pare is a desert night world in the Anagris system, perpetually dark with cold winds scouring a barren landscape. A mining colony was established here but failed due to hostile wildlife; its ruins remain, though nobody has reason to go back. [[Scrapjacks|Scrap Jack's Refueling and Grub]], a truck stop station, orbits nearby.
