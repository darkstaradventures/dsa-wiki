Generator-01, known as The Socket, is a black hole system in the Noctra system on the outskirts of the Viridian Expanse. The black hole's accretion disk represents a near-limitless source of energy.

Control of the system started the War for The Socket in 2435, fought between the sector's governments. About 40% of all human life was lost and every inter-governmental route was destroyed. Systems stayed dark and cut off for two centuries afterward. Small skirmishes in the war were derisively called "electroclashes." These background details come from the published sector guide and timeline.

In Episode 29, [[Felix Champlin]] hacked the Prometheon drill route directory and found an entry for "a place you've never heard of, a destination known as Generator 01." Felix remarked that it must be important if it was stored that deep, and he went on with the hack (Ep 29).
