The Vent is a local bar in [[The Spigot]] on [[Sibylen]], a quiet room of weary miners eating lunch or having a drink, with a blue-collar atmosphere.

Dr. Iso brought the crew here to find mechanic Peter Haslow, who was drinking at the bar. [[Thorne]] sat beside him under cover and paid for intel on an upcoming black-market shipment. A grenade was then thrown through the window; [[Robert Ross|Robert]] dove on it and tossed it behind the bar, but the bartender was killed in the blast. Iso pursued the attacker into an alley but lost him, and the bar was left partially destroyed, gas line igniting, as people evacuated (Ep 25).
