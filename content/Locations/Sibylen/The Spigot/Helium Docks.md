The Helium Docks are a loading zone on the outskirts of [[The Spigot]] on [[Sibylen]], between the town and the helium-3 mines. It's a collection of landing platforms circled with wire fencing, with a single wide opening barred by a security arm and card sensor. Three automated turrets target via infrared sentry cameras, and a speaker shouts warnings at anyone accessing the landing zone without proper clearance. Autonomous cargo shipments arrive at 3am; the ship lands, bots unload it within thirty minutes, and it departs, with no human personnel present.

The crew needed a black-market ownership module and staged a heist during the 3am autonomous shipment. A [[Viktor Strake]] clone attacked with drones during the raid, and Dr. Iso fought alongside the crew with her shotgun (Ep 27). The warehouse yielded a motorcycle, pharmaceuticals, and roughly 2,500 credits worth of goods.
