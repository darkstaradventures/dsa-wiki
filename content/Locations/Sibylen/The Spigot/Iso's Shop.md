Iso's Shop was Dr. Iso's clinic and black market shop in [[The Spigot]] on [[Sibylen]], a single-story concrete building in a less-traveled part of town, with a leaky air unit staining the wall nearby with rust. Inside, it was narrow but long, cluttered floor to ceiling with medical equipment, vials, potions, and disconnected electronics, with a long blue curtain across the back.

[[Haleyy]] led the crew here so Iso could scan her brain, revealing crystalline structures growing inside it (Ep 25). A Robert-line clone was later found dead outside the shop, killed by Iso's security turret, and the shop itself was burned down in an arson tied to [[Viktor Strake|Strake]]'s forces. Iso joined the crew after losing her shop (Ep 27).
