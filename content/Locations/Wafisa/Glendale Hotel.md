The Glendale Hotel is a seven-story hotel on [[Wafisa]] that has been completely ransacked. On the fourth floor, the crew found a barricaded room containing two dead bodies: Michael and Claire Harrow. Video, notes, and other information found here led the crew to [[17 Millbrook Lane]] and [[Millbrook Storage]].
