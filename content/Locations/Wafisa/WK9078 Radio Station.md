WK9078 is a two-story concrete radio station on [[Wafisa]] with a large radio antenna array on the roof. An obnoxious yellow sign above the front door reads "WK9078," and faded spray paint above it reads "The Voice of the Wastes." A small parking garage attached to the side is filled with abandoned cars.

It's the home and broadcast base of [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Reginald "Froggy" Vance]], who took over as DJ as a child after the fall of Waa and has been trying to get the station running for fifteen years. The ground floor holds a lobby with band and concert posters and a DJ booth behind bulletproof glass; the second floor has a kitchen, bathroom, and waiting area; the roof holds the antenna, reached by a ladder through a roof hatch; and the basement holds a generator, fuel cells, and electrical panels.

The crew found the station while exploring Wafisa and met Froggy, who had been alone for years broadcasting to no one (Ep 6). [[Felix Champlin|Felix]] assessed that repairing the radio tower would take two full days; the actual repair happened off-screen between episodes.
