The Switching Station is a three-room station south of [[Mistretta 4]] on [[Wafisa]] that controls the methane pipeline feeding [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]]'s research base — two stories, a front entrance only, and a window on the west side.

The [[Guardians of the Great Work]] had shut off the gas main to Voss's outpost, forcing her to run on extremely limited power and disconnect from the ExoNet. She asked the crew to restore the methane flow as their first task (Ep 3). The crew destroyed the switching station to do it, restoring methane flow to Mistretta 4 and denying it to the Guardians (Ep 4). While they were away, the Guardians broke into and stole [[The Scapegoat]].
