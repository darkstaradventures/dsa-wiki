Millbrook Storage is a ransacked storage facility on [[Wafisa]], near the [[17 Millbrook Lane|Harrow residence]]. Following instructions left by Michael Harrow on a USB recording device found at the [[Glendale Hotel]] — "There's a storage facility near my house, bay 17. I built a false wall right inside. Code 32 87." — the crew located bay 17.

Behind the false wall, the crew found [[The Aurora]] (a hover car), along with a leather jacket, tools, and a compact firearm.
