Wafisa is a mostly wild world with a single megacity, in the Revarmi Trita system, positioned over an enormous thermal gas vent. The megacity has two spaceports. Once incredibly prosperous from mining operations, it's now a ghost of its former self — something went wrong, and most people abandoned the city.

The climate is a balmy 72°F with constant drizzle under a pale green sky. The rain smells sharp and oily from methane in the upper atmosphere, feels slick on skin, and coats the streets with a rainbow sheen. Days run 36 hours with an 8-hour night. The ocean near [[Blackharbor]] is only slightly toxic to humans.

Wafisa is a post-collapse world, not a post-apocalyptic one. Seventy-two years ago, an explosion destroyed 90% of all mining operations — lines exploded or erupted into flames across the city, fires that still burn beneath the surface to this day. The collapse was caused by a squabble over gas rights involving several military forces and rebels. Every solvent company moved off-world, and the workers who couldn't afford to leave were abandoned. Those who remained formed the [[Guardians of the Great Work]].

The megacity was once home to millions; several hundred thousand remain among derelict streets and broken buildings. The Guardians have seized all energy sources — solar panels, generators, methane — for their own use. Anyone not living in Blackharbor and participating in their society has no energy and no means to generate it. Several companies maintain research bases here, drawn by the thermal vents, unlimited methane, and lack of laws. The world is also rich in ancient alien artifacts predating humanity's arrival in the sector, though at lower yields than [[Sibylen]].

Sub-locations include the coastal settlement of [[Blackharbor]], the abandoned [[Port Ascension]] spaceport, [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]]'s [[Mistretta 4|Omnitek research station]], the ransacked [[Glendale Hotel]], [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Froggy]]'s [[WK9078 Radio Station]], the Harrow family's home at [[17 Millbrook Lane]], the nearby [[Millbrook Storage]] facility, the [[Talas Gas Mines]], and [[The Switching Station]] that controls the methane pipeline.

Purple flower fields grow outside the megacity, which [[Haleyy]] set on fire in Ep 7.
