The Military Airfield sits on the northern edge of [[Blackharbor]] on [[Wafisa]], abutting the green ocean. Once much larger, it's now bombed out to a quarter of its original size, with three active landing strips remaining and roughly 2,500 Guardian soldiers stationed there.

[[The Scapegoat]] was taken here after being stolen, and the crew reclaimed their ship from the airfield in Ep 7, with [[Viktor Strake]] sending soldiers to trace them here.
