The Military Airfield sits inside the [[The Guardian Compound|Guardian Compound]] in [[Blackharbor]] on [[Wafisa]]. It wasn't originally built as an airfield: it's flanked by two rows of warehouses with a large open space between them, once grassy, now mostly churned to mud by spaceships and ground vehicles. Derelict, gutted spaceships sit at the north end.

[[The Scapegoat]] was taken here after being stolen, and the crew reclaimed their ship from the airfield in Ep 7, with [[Viktor Strake]] sending soldiers to trace them here.
