The Great Market is an open-air market in [[Blackharbor]] on [[Wafisa]] where dried fish, livestock, black-market tech, and handmade goods are traded under the watchful eye of [[Guardians of the Great Work|Guardian]] patrols. Hidden beneath the stalls and in basements, rebel messengers and traders whisper of resistance.
