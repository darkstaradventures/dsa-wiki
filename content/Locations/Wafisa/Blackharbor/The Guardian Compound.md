The Guardian Compound is the military base of the [[Guardians of the Great Work]] in [[Blackharbor]] on [[Wafisa]], half a mile square, a fortress of concrete and steel surrounded by walls, watchtowers, and automated turrets. Inside, well-fed officers plan the next round of taxation, conscription, and suppression.
