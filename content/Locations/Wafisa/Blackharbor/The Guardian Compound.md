The Guardian Compound is the military base of the [[Guardians of the Great Work]] in [[Blackharbor]] on [[Wafisa]], taking up about half of Blackharbor's total land area. It's walled in on three sides (with the fourth reaching out into the ocean), with walls roughly 22 to 24 feet tall and about 18 inches thick.
