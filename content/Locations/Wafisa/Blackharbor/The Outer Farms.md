The Outer Farms are struggling livestock ranches and hydroponic greenhouses on the outskirts of [[Blackharbor]] on [[Wafisa]]. The [[Guardians of the Great Work]] seize the best crops, and some residents secretly raise extra animals in underground pens to hide them from Guardian inspections.
