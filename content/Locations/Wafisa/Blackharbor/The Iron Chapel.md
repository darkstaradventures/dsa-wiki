The Iron Chapel is a former pre-collapse church in [[Blackharbor]] on [[Wafisa]], now serving as a makeshift hospital, shelter, and rebel meeting place. It's run by Brother Edric. The [[Guardians of the Great Work]] tolerate its existence, but rumors persist that the priest is aiding the resistance.
