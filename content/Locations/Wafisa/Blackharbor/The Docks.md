The Docks are the heart of [[Blackharbor]]'s industry on [[Wafisa]] — aquatic creature traps, fishing boats, and seaweed farms, all tightly controlled by the [[Guardians of the Great Work]], who levy high taxes on every haul. Smuggling rings operate in secret here, slipping extra fish to starving families.
