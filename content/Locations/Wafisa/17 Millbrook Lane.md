17 Millbrook Lane is the residence of the Harrow family on [[Wafisa]]. The crew found it by following instructions left by Michael Harrow on his laptop at the [[Glendale Hotel]]: an unsent email draft with the subject line "if someone finds this go to 17 Millbrook Lane" and body text reading "there's a book in the bottom drawer."

Following the instructions, the crew found the Lokipon Cards and the Harrow Family Cookbook here. The cookbook is a red notebook spanning three generations, with roughly a hundred blank pages in the back suggesting it may be more than just recipes.
