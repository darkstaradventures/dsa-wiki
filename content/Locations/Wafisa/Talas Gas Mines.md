The Talas Gas Mines are gas mines on [[Wafisa]], originally operated by a separatist colony. The methane pipeline from the mines was seized by the [[Guardians of the Great Work]] to fund their restoration project. The pipeline connects to [[The Switching Station]], which controls gas flow to locations including [[Mistretta 4]].
