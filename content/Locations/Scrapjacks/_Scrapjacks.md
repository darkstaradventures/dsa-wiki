Scrap Jack's Refueling and Grub is an interstellar truck stop and fuel depot in the Anagris system, orbiting near the desert world [[Pare]]. It has ten landing pads and serves as a waypoint for long-haul spacers, run by Yana 7 with a station AI named Buddy. Scrapjacks is actually a spaceship: it has its own spike drive, though incredibly expensive to operate, letting it relocate if needed.

Scrapjacks used to exist in the inner system closer to [[Polyphn]]. As more way stations and corporate-backed operations moved into the sector, independent stops like Scrapjacks couldn't compete, and it was cheaper to activate the spike drive and relocate than to fight, pushing the station further and further out over the years. Now it survives by catering to long-haulers, asteroid miners, and ice haulers bringing resources back to the inner systems.

The station enforces a strict no-weapons policy with scanning at the airlock, key-card access for dorms and showers, and just six security staff armed with only batons. It's currently nowhere near capacity, too far out on the fringes to draw much traffic.

The crew stopped here for fuel and found the station taken over by fifteen [[Void Cultists]] (Ep 10-11), who activated a void ansible that tore a rift in spacetime. With their weapons left behind per station policy, the crew had to retrieve them, save hostages, and stop the ritual. During the ordeal, [[Haleyy]] was touched by a void tendril and had a vision of surgeons and a worm-like machine, and station security officer Dan Grand was found in crew storage, corrupted and muttering void hymns: the first major appearance of the corrupted state known as Unhallowed. After the crew defeated the cultists, the station's mechanics fixed [[The Scapegoat]] for free.
