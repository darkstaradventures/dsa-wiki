Zephyrus Three is [[Thorne]]'s home planet, located in a distant system within the Viridian Expanse, known for its people's beauty. It's home of the Drax family and their shipping company, Draxus Goods, which operated for five generations before being seized by [[Nexus Trading]] through fraud.

William Sallerist infiltrated the business through a smuggling ring; Thorne's mother, Thalia Draxus, framed both Thorne and Sallerist, but Sallerist pinned everything on Thorne. Nexus Trading — owned by Prometheon Technologies, in turn owned by [[Virodyne Labs]] — took control of the business.

The worst-kept secret on Zephyrus Three was Thorne's need for his mother's approval. She was the dominant force in local commerce; there wasn't a deal made on the planet that didn't cross her desk. Thorne wanted to live up to her name while making one of his own, imagining a future where she would retire and pass control of Draxus Goods to him — a torch that never materialized. So he pushed harder, chasing larger deals and new avenues of business, hoping to transform the company into something greater than she'd ever imagined and earn the handoff. What started as a plan to make his mother proud became his greatest shame.
