New Australia, called Aussie 1, is a world in the [[_Oceanus|Oceanus]] system and the home world of [[Dr. Elara Voss]]. The published sector guide describes it as a backwater colony named for Earth's Australia.

[[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne Laboratories]] purchased the world. In Episode 8, Voss said Virodyne bought her settlement when she was a girl and employed everyone there, which made it a corporate town. The people had been in poverty on a backwater planet. Capable people were given jobs, mostly off-world, and she said things did not work out well for the people who stayed.

In Episode 21, [[Thorne]] recounted watching the auction for the [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] exosite. He said a Virodyne representative took a [[Novacrypt]] representative aside, and that he overheard something about "Aussie One and the Red Rice War." He could not hear the rest of the exchange, and the bidding ended soon after (Ep 21). The show did not explain the reference.
