Oceanus is a system at the southwestern edge of the Viridian Expanse with two inhabited worlds, [[New Australia]] (called Aussie 1) and New Hawaii. According to the published sector guide, Earth colonists settled both and named them after Pacific places on Earth. The company [[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne Laboratories]] purchased both worlds outright and employed their populations.

[[Dr. Elara Voss]] was born on New Australia. In Episode 8 she described how Virodyne bought her home settlement when she was a girl and made it a corporate town. She named the settlement a backwater, in poverty, where capable people were given mostly off-world jobs.

New Hawaii came up once in the episodes. In Episode 23, [[Thorne]] told the crew that their next stop would be New Hawaii, and [[Robert Ross|Robert]] answered "Ooh, new Hawaii." The show did not describe it further.
