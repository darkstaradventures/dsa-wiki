Laicar Research is a corporation focused on xenoarchaeology, primarily active on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]]. The corporation discovered Exosite 34b, the alien quarantine building that became central to the crew's business on Sibylen.

## Notable Members

Vera Corbin worked for Laicar as a xenoarchaeologist on Sibylen.

## Relationships

Laicar Research is a client of [[Scapegoat Solutions LLC]] under a legally binding contract. In Episode 21, [[Thorne]] used that contract as leverage, arguing the crew were technically Laicar employees to force [[Viktor Strake]] to release them for 24 hours. Laicar also competed at auction against [[Virodyne Labs]] and Novacrypt Solutions.
