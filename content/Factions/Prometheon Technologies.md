Prometheon Technologies is a subsidiary of [[Virodyne Labs]] specializing in dark-energy and antimatter research. Much of what Virodyne does in genetics is backed by Prometheon's research. The corporation's headquarters is a secret space station somewhere in deep space, its exact location classified. It has been described as interlocking rings, like a "Celestial Gyroscope," pierced by a long central rod.

The station briefly became visible to the crew through an alien relay on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]] when the alien Fork technology there was activated.

## Relationships

Prometheon is a subsidiary of [[Virodyne Labs]]. [[Dr. Elara Voss]], the VI who recruited the crew, has expressed hostility toward the station and its operations.
