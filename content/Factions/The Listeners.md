The Listeners are a masked group operating inside [[Prometheon Station]], identifiable by matching long black coats, gas masks, and thigh-high rubber boots, dressed as if for a hazardous environment. Members carry crowbars. Their leader, an older man in his fifties with silver hair, described the group as being "part of the same thread that runs throughout life," calling themselves "the listeners."

When the crew boarded the station (Ep 31), Listeners tracked them through the corridors and eventually confronted them near a barricaded section alongside a horde of [[Unhallowed Stages|Unhallowed]]. Their leader approached [[Haleyy]] directly, telling her "I know that you are already contacted with the void," and tried to recruit both her and [[Thorne]], insisting they were already "with us." Haleyy refused and knocked him unconscious. A firefight with other Listeners and combat drones followed in a sealed lab holding [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]].

Whether the Listeners answer to [[Viktor Strake]] or [[Prometheon Technologies]], or operate independently inside the station, is unconfirmed.
