The Listeners are a faction of gas-masked [[Void Cultists]] who run the labor and security side of [[Prometheon Station]] day to day, backed by an army of shambling [[Unhallowed Stages|Unhallowed]]. Identifiable by matching long black coats, gas masks, and thigh-high rubber boots, members carry crowbars. Their leader, [[Sabien]], described the group as being "part of the same thread that runs throughout life."

When the crew first boarded the station (Ep 31), Listeners tracked them through the corridors and eventually confronted them near a barricaded section alongside a horde of Unhallowed. Sabien approached [[Haleyy]] directly, telling her "I know that you are already contacted with the void," and tried to recruit both her and [[Thorne]], insisting they were already "with us." Haleyy refused and knocked him unconscious. A firefight with other Listeners and combat drones followed in a sealed lab holding [[Dr. Elara Voss|Voss]].

Across the time loop that followed (Ep 32), Sabien specifically singled out Haleyy and Thorne for recruitment on repeat attempts, dying twice in the process: once shot on sight after introducing himself, and once found already dead outside the station's admin wing, killed along with four other Listeners by [[Viktor Strake]] as an unsolicited favor to the crew, a note reading "you're welcome" left on the bodies. Whether the Listeners answer to Strake or [[Prometheon Technologies]], or operate independently inside the station, is unconfirmed.
