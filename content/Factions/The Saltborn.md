The Saltborn are the rebellion operating within [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]], a loose network of smugglers, dissidents, and saboteurs fighting against the rule of the [[Guardians of the Great Work]]. Their name reflects the belief that Blackharbor's people, born of salt and sea, should never bow to tyrants.

## Locations

The Iron Chapel serves as the Saltborn's meeting place, alongside hidden passages, market-stall basements, and other spaces throughout Blackharbor.

## Relationships

The crew of [[The Scapegoat]] aided the Saltborn during their time in Blackharbor. Joran Red Veyne is described as extremely charismatic and resourceful, having gathered other skilled people around him in the fight against the Guardians.
