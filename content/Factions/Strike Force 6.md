Strike Force 6 is the military unit that [[Robert Ross]] and the woman who became [[Haleyy]] served in before the events of the series. The transcripts render the name as "Strike Unit 6" (Ep 1, Ep 2) and "strike team six" (Ep 8); "Strike Force 6" does not appear verbatim.

Haleyy asks Robert whether a chaotic scene is "a normal Strike Unit 6 adventure," and he answers that it is the least weird thing they did (Ep 1). In Ep 8, after the crew is referred to as the Elara Voss crew, Robert complains that "they" have no respect for "blackout one or strike team six."

In Ep 19 Robert describes his squad on Ventos, which he calls a clone squadron: clones were made of him, and he claims he is the original. Haley with one Y was his commander. The members shown in the flashback were:
- Robert, who says his clone ID was RR7.
- Jackal, who wears an eye patch, uses a combat rifle and knife, and carries demolition charges.
- Envy, who is short-tempered and competes with the others over kills.
- Headcase, who perches above the cave entrance and stabs the guard alongside Jackal.

The squad's mission was to extract Haley from a cave outpost of the elites on [[_Ventos|Ventos]], where she was held and interrogated during the [[Red Rice Wars]]. Disguised as elites, they killed the guard and two interrogators, freed her, fought through the caves, and found an alien structure holding a fork-shaped device (see [[The Fork]]), which struck Haley with energy. Headcase died when his neck was snapped in the fall at the bottom of the cliff. On the way out, a helicopter carrying the survivors was shot down by an anti-air mech. Robert woke to find Jackal armless and Envy decapitated nearby, and Haley apparently dead (Ep 19 flashback). Haley is called commander throughout, and in Ep 8 the squad's final mission is described as one she commanded.

In Ep 8, [[Dr. Elara Voss|Dr. Voss]] presented footage and documents claiming the mission was a test, that the squad was an experiment, and that [[Omnitek Holdings]] and [[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne]] were complicit in Haley's death. Robert says that afterward he was put on desk duty, the clone program was scrapped, and he was kept from contact with his clones (Ep 19).
