The Viridian Expanse is dominated by a handful of interlocking mega-corporations, alongside independent operators, military powers, gangs, and cults that the crew of [[The Scapegoat]] has encountered in the course of their travels.

## Corporate Hierarchy

[[Virodyne Labs]] sits atop a hidden corporate empire, secretly controlling [[Prometheon Technologies]] (dark energy and antimatter research, which in turn controls [[Nexus Trading]]) and [[Omnitek Holdings]] (planetary mining and prisoner-contract labor). Neither subsidiary publicly advertises its connection to Virodyne.

## Independent Corporations

- [[Laicar Research]]: xenoarchaeology, based on [[_Sibylen|Sibylen]]
- Blackout Press: underground journalism, sector-wide
- [[Auctioneers Guild]]: neutral asset auctions, based on Sibylen
- [[Novacrypt]]: newly arrived, claimed [[Prometheon Station]] and its system at the end of Season 1

## Military / Political

- [[Guardians of the Great Work]]: military dictatorship controlling [[_Blackharbor|Blackharbor]] and the ruins of [[_Wafisa|Wafisa]]

## Gangs

- [[Drift Rats]]: roughly 28 members, [[_Camiri 4|Camiri 4]]
- [[Neon Fangs]]: roughly 85 members, Camiri 4

## Cults

- [[Void Cultists]]: seek "void ascension," encountered at [[_Scrapjacks|Scrapjacks]] and Sibylen
- [[The Listeners]]: gas-masked Void Cultist faction that ran Prometheon Station under [[Sabien]] until the [[The Time Loop|time loop]]'s end (Ep 33)

## The Crew

- [[Scapegoat Solutions LLC]]: the legal business entity founded by [[Thorne]] to give the crew standing in a corporation-dominated sector

## Known Relationships

- [[Virodyne Labs]] secretly controls [[Prometheon Technologies]] (and by extension [[Nexus Trading]]) and [[Omnitek Holdings]]
- [[Drift Rats]] and [[Neon Fangs]] are rivals for territory on Camiri 4
- [[Viktor Strake]], acting for Virodyne, pursued the crew of [[The Scapegoat]] since early in their travels, until his death at the end of Season 1 (Ep 33)
- [[Novacrypt]] has claimed [[Prometheon Station]] out from under [[Prometheon Technologies]] and [[Virodyne Labs]]
