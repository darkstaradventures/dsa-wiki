Novacrypt is a corporation that moved into [[_Prometheon Station|Prometheon Station]]'s system at the very end of Season 1, after a Trinity-class fleet cruiser picked up the energy readings of the station going critical and came to investigate. Finding the installation abandoned, well-equipped, and effectively undefended, Novacrypt swooped in, set up shop, and brought in more ships over the following days. When [[Virodyne Labs|Virodyne]] ships eventually arrived to check on their asset, they turned and left without firing a shot rather than challenge the Novacrypt fleet already in place.

Novacrypt now owns the system outright, including whatever [[Prometheon Technologies]] was building at the station, and appears to be working alongside [[The Concordant]], the newly-seated [[Virtual Intelligence|virtual intelligence]] running the station day to day. Rather than treat the crew of [[The Scapegoat]] as trespassers, Novacrypt offered them a legitimate contractor deal, buying out [[Thorne]], [[Reginald Froggy Vance|Reginald "Froggy" Vance]], and [[Felix Champlin|Felix]]'s outstanding arrest records in the process. [[Omnitek Holdings]], however, declined to release its claim on [[Robert Ross|Robert]] and [[Dr. Iso|Iso]] (its clones) or [[Haleyy]] (its "experimental psychic warrior") that easily.
