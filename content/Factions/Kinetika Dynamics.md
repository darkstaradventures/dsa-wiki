Kinetika Dynamics is a weapons and armor manufacturer operating as a subsidiary of [[Virodyne Labs]]. It keeps a low profile, working mostly in the background of Virodyne's operations. Its weapons technology was used in experiments on [[Dr. Elara Voss]]'s homeworld, and Kinetika-supplied weapons and armor equipped the experimental clone squadron — including [[Robert Ross]] — deployed to [[_Ventos|Ventos]].
