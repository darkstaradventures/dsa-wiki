Draxus Goods is the shipping company of [[Thorne]]'s family. In Ep 13, Thorne described it as family owned and operated for generations: "You name it, we deliver it." The business operated entirely on [[Zephyrus Three]], Thorne's home world (Ep 10, Ep 13). His mother ran it. Thorne wanted to grow it into an interstellar business, and she refused, citing risk and lack of funds. Thorne is the heir and goes by Thorne Draxus (Ep 18, Ep 21).

Hoping a corporation would help him expand, Thorne partnered with [[William Sallerist]] of [[Nexus Trading]] (Ep 13). Sallerist was running a smuggling ring. Thorne was framed for smuggling and sent to prison, and Sallerist took over the company, so the family lost the business and disowned Thorne (Ep 13). Years later, Thorne's mother told him that she was the one who had implicated him alongside Sallerist. She said she judged his deals a growing risk to the company and wanted both men removed, but she had not expected Sallerist to pin everything on Thorne. Sallerist then used the power of Nexus to take over the company, which she said took five generations to build and ten years to lose. She had spent the years since trying to reclaim it, and she gave Thorne his father's wedding ring, inscribed with the Drax family name. Thorne promised that the company would be owned by the family again (Ep 13).

[[Dr. Elara Voss]] told the crew that [[Prometheon Technologies]] owns Nexus, "the corporation that stole and gutted your family's business" (Ep 8). [[Viktor Strake|Strake]] later mocked Thorne as an expert at crumbling his family's dynasty on Zephyrus III (Ep 21). When the GM called Thorne heir to the Draxus Trading Company, Thorne corrected it to Draxus Goods (Ep 18). The Nexus agent [[Rynn Kade]] said Nexus had stolen Thorne's company from him and that Nexus's higher-ups knew who he was (Ep 24). In Ep 13, Thorne said he hoped everyone back on Zephyrus was all right. The show does not say what became of the company afterward.
