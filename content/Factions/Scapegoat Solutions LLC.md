Scapegoat Solutions LLC is the crew's own legal business entity, created by [[Thorne]] using his knowledge of corporate law. It serves as their legal identity in a sector dominated by corporations, giving them standing to make contracts and hold property, and to negotiate on equal footing with the megacorporations around them.

## Members

- [[Thorne]]: Founder / legal representative
- [[Haleyy]]
- [[Robert Ross]]
- [[Felix Champlin]]

## Legal Uses

In Episode 17, Thorne used InterCorporate policy to argue that the crew were technically [[Laicar Research]] employees under a legally binding contract with Scapegoat Solutions LLC, forcing [[Viktor Strake]] to release them for 24 hours.
